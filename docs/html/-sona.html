-sona

-sona is a clipped suffix of persona which is used to alter a noun to describe its use as a representation of yourself. For example, a chairsona would be a person's representation as a chair. -sonas tend to be used for one's representation within a fandom, a self-insert OC, and/or a character for roleplay. The most notable use of -sona is in fursona, a persona based around a furry.
