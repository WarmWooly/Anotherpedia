1,847 lemons

1,847 lemons refers to a copypasta and fake news story of a Canadian woman sending 1,847 orders of a single lemon through DoorDash to her ex. It would become a meme due to the absurdity of such a tactic to get back at an ex, especially with the high cost and extensive planning required to undertake such a feat. The person in the mugshot is unrelated to the story. Posts about the story usually highlight that it is the first-ever "DoorDash psychological warfare" case, intending to make the ex fearful of the sender.
