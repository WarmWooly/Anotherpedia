Aide (Bits & Bops)

The aide or assistant is a suit-wearing man in Bits & Bops, appearing within the minigame/mixtapes President Bird, Fire Mixtape, and Final Mixtape. He directly tells Mr. President what to say through a black handheld radio. The aide has fair skin with gray hair and brown eyes. He wears a gray suit with a white undershirt and a black necktie, alongside a pair of dark gray glasses. In the Fire Mixtape, the aide is visibly sweating and is tinted orange.
