Anno (Anotherpedia)

<>
Anno is an original character of a flow worm from Bits & Bops created by Warm_Wooly on March 20, 2026 and uploaded to Anotherpedia the following day. Anno is a play on Wikipedia's Wikipede while representing how popular the page on flow worms had been on Anotherpedia at the time of creation. He is currently used on Anotherpedia's original character, character reference sheet, two-frame animation, and spinning object meme pages as examples. For more images, see Gallery of Anno (Anotherpedia).
Anno nearly matches flow worms in appearance, besides having his bottom legs at the end of his abdomen. Anno's color is based on the Anotherpedia logo. It's a coincidence that he matches Anotherpedia's colors and that he likes Anotherpedia. He can't glow, and his body is surprisingly rigid for a flow worm, making it hard for him to stretch. He likes reading the various pages on Anotherpedia. He's probably reading this right now! He doesn't like to make or edit pages, but he swears on his antennae that he will eventually.

Anno is always moving, at least a bit, every 0.3 seconds. He can't help it; it's just how he's animated. His design is inspired by skribbl.io. He hasn't played Bits & Bops ironically, but he "likes the design of the characters" or something. I don't know if it's a meta commentary on original characters or if he's just messing with me. Anno is glad that flow worms (the species) are more popular than flow worms (the fishing lure), but he's worried that an angler will accidentally confuse him for the lure. He prefers sweet fruits over meat.

While Anno enjoys all the pages on Anotherpedia, he tends to have a sweet spot for older pages. He seems to stalk the timeline to catch when new pages are made. His favorite minigame is Pageman, since he's tried to memorize all the pages, but he also gets a good chuckle out of Hate or Date. He has pretended to have read the disclaimer. Anno strictly dislikes emojis used on Anotherpedia pages because they detract from the ✨aesthetic✨. Unsafe content doesn't really faze him, but he prefers pages covering very niece topics.

Anno hasn't met Kip because Anno doesn't have a passport to travel to the United Kingdom. He knows about Blaze and Romi through their Anotherpedia pages. He's never been mistaken for an ant, but rather as a cat and a donkey, surprisingly. He says he's not a wormsona of the Anotherpedia logo.
