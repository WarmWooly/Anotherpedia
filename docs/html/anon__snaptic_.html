Anon (Snaptic)

Anon is the player character in the upcoming puzzle-platformer Snaptic. He is voiced by Corey LeVier and first appeared in "glass memory". Anon has a chrome body with rounded arms, legs, and a floating spherical head. He has hidden joints at his shoulders, hips, elbows, and knees, allowing for articulation. He can also collapse his entire body, rolling around as just his head. Anon can run and jump. As Anon is magnetic, he is attracted to magnetic surfaces while rolling around as his head. He can also use a drag-selection box and the windows of other programs. Anon wakes up on the laptop and traverses through some starting area (likely the data hall). While passing through, he meets Valence. Continuing forward, he enters the DVD drive and talks to Auralyn.
