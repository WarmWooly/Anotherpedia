Anotherpedia logo

Not to be confused with Anotherpedia log.
The Anotherpedia logo is the logo of Anotherpedia, being comprised of three short cylinders using the grayish-blue the webpage's color scheme. It was made in Google Drawings while the website was first being developed, primarily designed to be both easily recognizable and very simple. It was then updated on February 24, 2024, improving the image quality and adding an outline while converting it from .ico to .svg. The logo most prominently appears as the favicon for Anotherpedia and the button to return to the main page on mobile.

The logo for Anotherpedia appears on several pages, generally being a representation of Anotherpedia as a whole. It also appears on notices and other warnings for the disclaimer. It appears on pages related to Anotherpedia, such as the Anotherpedia wishlist holding a wish list, the list of original characters on Anotherpedia as "skinny Anotherpedia", and the list of pages related to Anotherpedia with an arrow pointing back to itself. It is also used as examples on some pages, including a splode where the logo a splodes and pixel art where the logo is assembled.

The Anotherpedia logo is occasionally personified, taking the form of the logo holding onto or interacting with something. The Anotherpedia logo is distinct from Anno, who serves as the original character "mascot" for Anotherpedia. Anno is not a wormsona of Anotherpedia or its logo.
