Ants (Bits & Bops)

The ants are ants in Bits & Bops, appearing within the minigame/mixtapes Pantry Parade, Jungle Mixtape, and Final Mixtape. They are shown carrying food around. The ants are red in color, with a large head and abdoment and a small thorax. The ants only have four limbs, with two legs and two arms. Their hands and feet are maroon in color. They have dot eyes and a large mouth, with a pair of short antennae atop their heads. In the Jungle Mixtape, they are yellow-orange in color with orange hands and feet.
