Are you so busy fighting you cannot see your own ship has set sail?

"Are you so busy fighting you cannot see your own ship has set sail?" is a quote spoken by Uncle Iroh during the ninth episode of the first season of Avatar: The Last Airbender, The Waterbending Scroll. Before, the pirate captain and Prince Zuko are fighting each other over capturing Aang, eventually getting so caught up that they do not see that Aang and his friends have escaped. Iroh breaks up the fight, pointing to the captain and saying the line. Zuko snaps back, saying that it's not a good time for his proverbs, with Iroh reiterating that it is literal. The pirate captain notices his ship has set sail, stolen by Aang and his friends, with Zuko laughing at him. Shortly after, Zuko notices that the other pirates have commandeered his ship. Iroh then comments that maybe it should be a proverb.
