B-Bot (Bits & Bops)

B-Bot is a robot in Bits & Bops, appearing within the minigame/mixtapes B-Bot and the Fly Girls, Sky Mixtape, and Final Mixtape. He also appears on the title screen. He dances alongside the Fly Girls. B-Bot has a yellow, rectangular body with pairs of arms, legs, and a neck connecting to a head, with a blue circle in the middle. His arms and legs are blue tubes, with yellow gloves and shoes for hands and feet. His head is also rectangular, with circular protrusions on the sides, a long antenna on top, and two screens for his eyes and mouth. While B-Bot's eye screen is fixed in size, going across his entire face, his mouth screen can slightly change shape. His screen is green while they are doing well, yellow when he slightly messes up, and red when he completely fumbles. In the Sky Mixtape, B-Bot is silver with blue-gray limbs, and his screen is orange while doing well.
