Baton (Bits & Blocks)

The baton is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that allows the player to view the instrument and note of a note block. The baton can be crafted with 1 Conductor and 1 note block (shapeless). When the baton is held, an overlay will appear to the right of the crosshair when over a note block, showing its current instrument and note.
