Battery tab

A battery tab (also called a battery pull tab or battery insulator tab) is a thin plastic or paper tab placed between an electric battery and a terminal in a product. Battery tabs are used to prevent the battery from draining in the device while in storage or shipping. It's designed with a grippable end (usually enlarged) to pull the tab from between the battery and terminal, allowing the user to connect the device with minimal hassle.
