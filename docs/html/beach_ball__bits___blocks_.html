Beach Ball (Bits & Blocks)

The beach ball is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that spawns a smackable entity. The beach ball can be crafted with 1 Flipper Snapper and 1 slimeball (shapeless). When used, the beach ball will be placed. If the player is sprinting when used, the ball will be thrown. Beach balls are used in the following crafting recipes:
Mangrove Apple - 1 beach ball, 1 Jungle Mixtape, and 1 apple (shapeless).
Party Ball - 1 beach ball, 1 Final Mixtape, and 1 paper (shapeless). When the beach ball is struck, it will be flung in the direction the attacker is facing. While it does have 20 health, it heals itself fully every tick. To kill the beach ball, the attack would need to do 20 or more damage at once. The beach ball will also float in water and lava.

The beach ball is immune to fire, fall damage, cacti, drowning, lightning, potion effects, anvils, dragon breath, and Wither. If the beach ball crams or suffocates, it will instantly despawn and drop itself.

The beach ball can be picked up by sneaking while interacting with it. gal
