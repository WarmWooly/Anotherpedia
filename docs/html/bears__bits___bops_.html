Bears (Bits & Bops)

The bears are anthropomorphic polar bears in Bits & Bops, appearing within the minigame/mixtapes Steady Bears, Sky Mixtape, and Final Mixtape. The boy and girl bears also appear in the bear snowglobe souvenir and on the title screen. The player-controlled boy is skating with a girl, with other skating bears appearing in Steady Bears. The bears stand upright, with white fur and lighter/darker patches on the insides of their ears and around their mouths. They have a mouth connected to a dark nose, with eyes and eyebrows. They wear thick fur-trimmed coats with matching pants. The fur trimming is cream in color. They wear ice skates and matching gloves. The boy wears a cyan coat and pants with blue skates and gloves, while the girl wears a magenta coat and pants with purple skates and gloves. Other NPCs wear hats alongside their attire, matching their coats and pants.

In the Sky Mixtape, the bears have whiter fur with blue patches and facial features, alongside a white fur trim on their coats. The boy has a soft blue coat and pants with pale cyan skates and gloves, while the girl has a pink coat and pants with soft purple skates and gloves.
