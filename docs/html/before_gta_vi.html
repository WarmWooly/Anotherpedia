Before GTA VI

Before {{iGTA VI}}, or more fully, "We got {{iX before GTA VI}}", was a meme format and phrase based on the long development of Grand Theft Auto VI, with large or unlikely events happening before its release on November 19, 2026. It would be especially popular on social media from 2023 onward as delays for the widely anticipated release kept happening. The format would be changed nearing the release, talking about its retirement, as after its release nothing can happen before GTA VI. On release day, jokes to revitalize the phrase, such as "We got GTA VI before X", were made.
