Bit (Bits & Bops)

Bit is an anthropomorphic cat in Bits & Bops and one of the two shopkeepers alongside Bop. She serves as a mascot for the game, appearing throughout menus and promotional material. Bit is a dark blue cat who stands upright. She has four fingers, rounded feet, and a long, curled tail. She has a short snout with a blue nose, a smooth W-shaped mouth, two large green eyes, and lines on her cheeks. Bit's ears are large and pointy, usually facing upward.

Bit is prominent throughout Bits & Bops, running the store during the postgame.
