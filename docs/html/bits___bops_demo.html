Bits & Bops demo

The {{iBits & Bops demo}} was a 2022 demo for the rhythm game Bits & Bops, featuring three minigames for the main game. The three minigames originally included are Flipper Snapper, Hammer Time!, and Meet & Tweet. The demo would add the minigame B-Bot and the Fly Girls for the 2024 Steam Next Fest.

The demo did not have any mixtapes and was only set in the minigame room. Many features, such as the tutorials, Diamond Perfects, and snarky text would be included, but all collectibles (souvenirs, records, videos, and pins) would not be awarded. It would also not include the safety video or the Perfect Tracker. When beating a minigame, a more traditional results screen would appear instead of the postcards that appear in the final game. The cover for Meet & Tweet and the title screen for B-Bot and the Fly Girls would be updated between the demo and full release.
