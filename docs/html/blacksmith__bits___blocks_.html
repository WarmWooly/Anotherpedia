Blacksmith (Bits & Blocks)

Blacksmith is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks based on the cartridge of the same name. It can be displayed on a mixtape stand. Blacksmith can be crafted with 1 minigame case and 1 iron sword (shapeless). Blacksmith is used in the following crafting recipes:
Forging Hammer - 1 Blacksmith, 1 iron ingot, and 1 stick (shapeless).
