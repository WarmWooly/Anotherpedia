Blacksmith (Bits & Bops)

Blacksmith is the third cartridge in the game corner in Bits & Bops, following the Ocean Mixtape. Up to 4 players can join as local multiplayer, where they are kobolds blacksmithing. Each player controls a kobold, pressing Button 1 to hit the metal to work it. A metronome will play with the pattern the player(s) must follow. The pattern must be played four times before the item is complete. If they mess up during the smithing, the item will poof. After all items are forged, the BPM increases. If the player(s) make all the available items (horseshoe, shield, sword, axe, flail, spatula, staff, and D20), then they are awarded the pin Smithy. Attempting the cartridge unlocks President Bird.
