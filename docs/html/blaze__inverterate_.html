Blaze (Inverterate)

Blaze is a flow worm OC created by Inverterate, which was first posted to the Discord server for Bits & Bops alongside Romi on July 14, 2026. His design is partially inspired by a theoretical Fire Mixtape flow worm. For more images, see Gallery of Blaze (Inverterate).
Blaze has a dark gray body with orange limbs/face and blue eyes/mouth. He glows a bright orange, as seen in an Easter egg for flow worms in Bits & Blocks. He also has a blue, 3-pointed fire symbol on their back.

In his first appearance, he is seen talking to Romi. Blaze is considered to be hyperactive and is an aromantic asexual. Blaze explores and brings back interesting things he finds to Romi. He can explode when he is in danger or injured, and is willing to put himself in harm's way for others. Blaze dismisses Romi's halo, feeling that it's symbolism is not as important as Romi being himself. He also seems to know Anno. He likes to eat hotdogs and churros, but occasionally nibbles on himself.
