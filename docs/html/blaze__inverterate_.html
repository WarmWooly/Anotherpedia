Blaze (Inverterate)

Blaze is a flow worm OC created by Inverterate, which was first posted to the Discord server for Bits & Bops alongside Romi on July 14, 2026. For more images, see Gallery of Blaze (Inverterate).
Blaze has a dark gray body with orange limbs/face and blue eyes/mouth. They glow a bright orange, as seen in an Easter egg for flow worms in Bits & Blocks. In their first appearance, they are seen talking to Romi. Blaze is considered to be hyperactive.
