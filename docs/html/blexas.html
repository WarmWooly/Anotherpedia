Blexas

"Blue Texas" redirects here. For other terms relating to Blue Texas, see Blue Texas (disambiguation).
Blexas or Blue Texas is the idea of Democrat control in the US state Texas, primarily through control of the House, Senate, and governor positions. It can also be used to refer to a general shift towards Democrat control within Texas, which is the most populous Republican-held state <>.

The term would be heavily used during the 2026 United States election, in which a blue wave caused Texas Republicans to lose their Senate and governor positions despite gerrymandering the year before to hold control. This would be signified by James Talarico (D) being elected Ken Paxton (R) for the Senate position and Gina Hinojosa (D) being elected over Greg Abbott (R) for the governor position. Texas Democrats would also gain one seat in the House of Representatives in the election.
