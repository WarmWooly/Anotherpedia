Blueprint (Bits & Blocks)

The blueprint is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that allows the player to continue placing blocks in the direction they are facing. The blueprint can be crafted with 1 instruction sheet, 1 Ocean Mixtape, and 1 blue dye (shapeless) When the instruction sheet is used on a block, it will attempt to place another in the direction the player is facing up to 10 blocks away. If the player does not have the same block in their inventory or the direction is already filled with blocks, nothing will happen. Sneaking while using it will change between Horizontal and Vertical mode. In Vertical mode, blocks will be placed downwards if the target is below the player and vice versa.
