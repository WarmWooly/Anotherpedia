Bop (Bits & Bops)

For the file format, see BOP file ({{aiBits & Bops).}}
Bop is an anthropomorphic dog in Bits & Bops and one of the two shopkeepers alongside Bit. He serves as a mascot for the game, appearing throughout menus, minigames, promotional material, and as the game's icon. Bop is a tan, rotund dog who stands upright. He has four fingers, three toes, and a small, round tail. He has a short snout with a dark nose, a smooth W-shaped mouth, two large eyes, and blush on his cheeks. Bop's ears are large and floppy, resting on the sides of his head. He has a round, lighter-colored patch on his belly.

Bop is prominent throughout Bits & Bops, running the store and helping the player when they get stuck on a minigame or mixtape.
