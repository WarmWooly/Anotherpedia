Bright Empty Spool (Not Lazy Chainmail)

The bright empty spool is an item in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail used to store multiple items together as spools for bulk winding. While nearly working the same as an empty spool, its inclusion mirrors the bright variations on spool blocks. Bright empty spools can be crafted with 4 empty spools and 1 glowstone dust (place the glowstone dust in the center and surround it with the empty spools; makes 4). Bright empty spools can be obtained when a Crusher crushes bright spool blocks. The bright empty spool can be haunted from an empty spool. Bright empty spools are used in the following crafting recipes:
Bright Copper Spool - 1 bright empty spool and 8 copper ingots (place the spool in the middle and place the ingots around it).
Bright Gold Spool - 1 bright empty spool and 8 gold ingots (place the spool in the middle and place the ingots around it).
Bright Iron Composite Spool - 1 bright empty spool and 8 iron composite ingots (place the spool in the middle and place the ingots around it).
Bright Iron Spool - 1 bright empty spool and 8 iron ingots (place the spool in the middle and place the ingots around it).
Bright Netherite Spool - 1 bright empty spool and 2 netherite ingots (place the spool in the middle and place the ingots above and below it).
Bright Rose Gold Spool - 1 bright empty spool and 8 rose gold ingots (place the spool in the middle and place the ingots around it).
Bright Spool Block - 1 bright empty spool and 2 wooden slabs (place the spool in the middle and place the slabs above and below it). Bright empty spools are used in the following melding recipes:
Crude Engine - 1 bright empty spool, 1 leather/kelp industrial belt, and 1 piston A bright empty spool can be smoked in a smoker to produce an empty spool, taking 1 second. In a furnace, blast furnace, or smoker, 1 bright empty spool can fuel for 5 seconds (0.5 items), with a maximum of 1 stack (64 bright empty spools, 320 seconds, 32 items).
