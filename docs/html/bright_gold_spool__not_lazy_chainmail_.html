Bright Gold Spool (Not Lazy Chainmail)

The bright gold spool is an item in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail used to be wound into gold coils, acting as the bulk alternative to the gold wire. Bright gold spools can be crafted with 1 bright empty spool and 8 gold ingots (place the spool in the middle and surround it with the ingots). Bright gold spools can be obtained when a Crusher crushes bright gold spool blocks. Bright gold spools can be haunted from gold spools. Bright gold spools are used in the following crafting recipes:
Bright Gold Spool Block - 1 bright gold spool and 2 wooden slabs (place the spool in the middle and place the slabs above and below it).
Bright Netherite Spool - 1 bright gold spool and 8 netherite scrap (surround the spool in scrap). Bright gold spools can be used on a winding jig or in an auto winder to be turned into 10 gold coils.
