Bright Iron Spool (Not Lazy Chainmail)

The bright iron spool is an item in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail used to be wound into iron coils, acting as the bulk alternative to the iron wire. Bright iron spools can be crafted with 1 bright empty spool and 8 iron ingots (place the spool in the middle and surround it with the ingots). Bright iron spools can be obtained when a Crusher crushes bright iron spool blocks. Bright iron spools can be haunted from iron spools. Bright iron spools are used in the following crafting recipes:
Bright Iron Spool Block - 1 bright iron spool and 2 wooden slabs (place the spool in the middle and place the slabs above and below it). Bright iron spools can be used on a winding jig or in an auto winder to be turned into 10 iron coils.
