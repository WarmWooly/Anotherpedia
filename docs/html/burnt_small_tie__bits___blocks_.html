Burnt Small Tie (Bits & Blocks)

The burnt small tie is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that applies buffs to a parrot. The burnt small tie can be crafted with 1 small tie and 1 Fire Mixtape (shapeless). When the burnt small tie is used on a parrot, it will consume the tie and permanently apply Health Boost II (+4 hearts), Regeneration, and Fire Resistance. The burnt small tie can't be applied if one is already on the parrot.
