Caterpillar

A caterpillar is a larva of a butterfly or moth, which is noted for its long, worm-like appearance. Their diet is species-dependant, with most feeding on plants (particurally leaves). After getting enough body mass, they will undergo metamorphosis as a pupa in a chrysalis (butterflies) or cocoon (moths) before emerging as adult insects.
