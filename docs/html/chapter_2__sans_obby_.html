Chapter 2 (Sans Obby)

<>
{{iChapter 2: Sans Incremental}} is the second chapter of the Roblox game Sans Obby. Chapter 2's development is on hiatus as a result of Roblox's new publishing guidelines. <>, this page's information is based on developer plans and current development progress. After the events of Chapter 1, the Player exits the Garganta into the room behind the office of Reactor 174 at 12:47 (12:47 PM). The ambient environmental temperature of the Reactor is -1°C (30°F). Everything is dark, getting darker and darker as night begins to fall. The office has a single desk with a lamp, a potted plant, a phone that only receives calls, a small fan, and a Teto Plush on it. In order to use the computer, the Player must pay 10 Energy every time they want to use it. As planned, at around 18:00 (6:00 PM), the Mysterious Stranger Over The Intercoms from Chapter 1 will send out the Night 1 phone call. Currently, only the phone calls for Nights 1 through 4 have been written out and voice-acted. This is the transcript for the Night 1 phone call taken directly from the original script:"Hello. Welcome to your brand-new job at the reactor complex. As your boss, I have taken to pre-recording a message to help you get settled in on your first night here at the facility. As long as you don’t do anything stupid, you should be fine. Your job is to collect the energy off that platform outside of your office. Take the [left] door [in front of you], and it will be the first door to the [Right]. Then you press the button on the board [in the room behind you] and our automated systems will sell it off, of which you will generate revenue that can be spent on things in this facility to improvement and provide other benefits. For entertainment, please consider making do with what you have access to. Walk around the facility, talk to things we have here and pretend they can talk back, you get the idea. For food and drink, feel free to head to the cafeteria and get something from the back room. If something should break during your time here, the parts and services room should be located at [the door on the right wall of your office]. To deter you from breaking things, parts are incredibly expensive. Also, some things you can order from within the facility do require parts as payment. For your own personal enrichment, as a very generous gift, you have been given by me [1] [potted plant]."
During the first night, only two threats are initially active. Those threats are Teto Plush and UnderGuy Sans. <>, there were no concrete story plans for these nights. Below is a table of the coded aggression values for the threats <> for these nights.<> The final night arrives. Like small animals in a forest, all the other threats have gone silent. The Player needs to survive for a single hour against PRIN, who is in actuality one of the 7 Evils of Humanity. PRIN represents the sin of ego/applause. This in-game hour is planned to pass at a 1:1 ratio of time in real life. At the end of this time, it is planned for the mysterious stranger from over the intercoms from Chapter 1, real identity "Mr. Reiuji", to show up and ultimately save the Player from PRIN with the aid of Longinus. After which, Mr. Reiuji will officially recognize your worth and allow you to make use of his facilities for your efforts in tracking down Bara O. Bama. As time passes, the Player will grow colder and colder and must manage their body temperature by cooling down and heating up. Heating up is primarily done by remaining inside the reactor room where Energy spawns. However, staying here for too long will cause the Player to slowly be roasted alive. Naturally, if the Player remains too cold, they will freeze to death. The way the Player is initially able to track their temperature is through the overlays that slowly transition from being invisible to visible. Later on, the Player is able to purchase an upgrade to view the Temperature Gauge from PRIN for 150 TAS (s Teto Abuse Score Totally Awesome Score!). With this gauge, the Player can visualize how hot or cold they are through the image of the fire and ice wolves on the bar, with AGL LR Glass Colder to denote the far cold side of the bar and STR LR Glass Warmer to denote the far hot side of the bar.

The Player must also pay attention to the Insight GUI, which will allow them to manage their heart rate, hunger, and thirst in the early game. It also gives pieces of lore and ambience as well. The first 2 rows of the Insight GUI are for the Player's random thoughts and primary thoughts, respectively. Underneath those two are the rows for the messages pertaining to the Player's hunger, thirst, and heart rate.<>
Some places also have special insight messages that take the place of the generic ones you get.<>
<> The player can sustain wounds. Wounds come in two types: light and heavy. While wounds are sustained, the Player will bleed. Bleeding decreases the Blood Gauge, which will result in death once empty. Bleeding will also cause the Player to leave behind a trail of blood. This blood is intended to be used as part of the stealth mechanic of the game. As a part of ambience, blood left behind will slowly begin to freeze as time passes in any room that is not the Reactor Room. The player will only leave behind blood during a wound tick. The way wound ticks are determined is first by calculating the BPS (Beats Per Second) of the player's heart rate; then, if the Player is not in PRIN's Spot, the game will then check to see if the Player has any light or heavy wounds. If the player does have any, the game will then calculate how much blood to use and generate blood puddles that will be on the floor under the Player. Once this is done, the game will increment a counter that, when reaching above 250, will heal one light wound. Light wounds can be healed through this ticking and from PRIN's shop for 25 TAS. Heavy wounds are only able to be healed from PRIN's shop for 200 TAS. When it comes to managing hunger and thirst, the player has a couple different options. Towards the Show Stage are the cafeteria and the kitchen. Inside the kitchen, the player is able to engage with the cooking mechanic and generate drinking water. The player is then able to eat cooked meals and drink drinking water inside the cafeteria. The other way is through the vending machines throughout the facility. Inside the Reactor Room, there are two vending machines. A snack vending machine with a small mini-game to play at the cost of 0.25 Money. This snack vending machine will scream with all inputs, which can attract unwanted attention. Once the mini-game is completed, the player is rewarded with an increase to their hunger that the vending machine script will calculate with some degree of randomness. The minimum possible hunger you will gain is 9. In that same room, there is a Bepis machine. The Bepis machine is intended to allow the player to purchase a variety of drinks at a variety of prices. The drink options are the following: クリスタル BEPIS, マキシマム BEPIS, ベピス BEPIS, ハイパー・リーサル BEPIS, and ブラック BEPIS. The prices are 140円, 150円, 120円, 210円, and 180円, respectively. The conversion rate is 0.08 Money per yen. Both food and drinks may be purchased from the Hatsune Miku-themed vending machine located inside Parts and Services. In addition to food and drink, the Player may also gamble with their life and safety with the third option of the vending machine: "Neru's Possibly Evil Sinister 3rd Thing". This option, when purchased, will roll from a table and apply an effect based on what is rolled. This vending machine also has a limited stock of everything that refreshes every night. There is a stock of 10 Miku Drinks, 5 Teto Meals, and 8 Neru's Possibly Evil Sinister 3rd Things. Throughout the facility, there are hiding spots. These hiding spots, among other factors, increase the Camo Index of the Player. This Camo Index is planned to be used as part of the stealth mechanic of the game. Things like running, having a high heart rate, and breathing can decrease this Camo Index, whereas sneaking and hiding increase it.
