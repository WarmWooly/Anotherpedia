Chef (Bits & Bops)

The chef is a chef in Bits & Bops, appearing within the minigame/mixtapes Pop-Up Kitchen, Ocean Mixtape, and Final Mixtape. They throw skewers and toothpicks to catch food. In the minigame and mixtapes, only the arm of the chef is visible. She has light skin with a large ponytail and face framing layers. She wears a white uniform with a white cap and a darker apron. It is implied that she is new to her job during the tutorial of Pop-Up Kitchen.
