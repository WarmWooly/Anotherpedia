cock is ticking...🤬⏱️ is a 2025--26 internet meme derived from the white rabbit pointing at a clock meme. It features a white rabbit sitting on a brown couch next to a clock. Its most popular variation has black text in Papyrus which reads "cock is ticking...🤬⏱️" along the top, with the image slightly squished vertically. It plays off of a simple typo between clock and cock, aided by the absurd imagery of the rabbit giving a side-eye with the small clock. A common addition is a thin, hand-drawn 'L' between 'c' and 'o' to form "cLock", continuing to play off the intentional typo.
