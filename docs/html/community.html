Community

A community is a group of people who share one or more commonalities, such as location, culture, religion, and values. Communities may describe themselves as such, or it may be a label applied to a group externally. A community has members who are socially active with each other, communicating information that pertains to their commonalities (such as students talking about their school). There are no bounds for a community, but communities are common when describing people in relation to a town or city. Communities can also be virtual, using online communication to keep connected. The size of communities is also variable, ranging from less than a dozen to hundreds of thousands of people.
