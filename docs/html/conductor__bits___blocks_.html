Conductor (Bits & Blocks)

Conductor is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks based on the cartridge of the same name. It can be displayed on a mixtape stand. Conductor can be crafted with 1 minigame case, 1 slimeball, and 1 stick (shapeless). Conductor is used in the following crafting recipes:
Baton - 1 Conductor and 1 note block (shapeless).
