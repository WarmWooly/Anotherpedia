Conductor (Bits & Bops)

Conductor is the first cartridge in the game corner in Bits & Bops, following the Jungle Mixtape. Up to 4 players can join as local multiplayer, where they try to conduct a frog orchestra. Each player controls part of the orchestra, pressing Button 1 to conduct. If Up is pressed, then a metronome will play. If the controlled conductor is going to the beat of the metronome, a cyan streak will appear at the end of their baton. If they mess up during the song, the streaks will only be white. If the player(s) reach the end without messing up, then they are awarded the pin Maestro. Attempting the cartridge unlocks B-Bot and the Fly Girls.
