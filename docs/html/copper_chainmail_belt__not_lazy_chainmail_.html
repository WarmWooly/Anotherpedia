Copper Chainmail Belt (Not Lazy Chainmail)

The copper chainmail belt is a belt curio in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail that applies Dolphin's Grace III and Conduit Power while in rain or water. The copper chainmail belt can be created through maille crafting with the settings "Copper Belt". The belt takes 128 copper chain links to craft. When the copper chainmail belt is worn in a belt slot and the wearer is in rain or water, the wearer will gain Dolphin's Grace III and Conduit Power. Copper chainmail belts can be crushed into unfinished copper projects containing 128 chain links. gal
