Copper Chainmail Bracelet (Not Lazy Chainmail)

The copper chainmail bracelet is a bracelet curio in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail that heals and buffs the player when struck by lightning. The copper chainmail bracelet can be created through maille crafting with the settings "Copper Bracelet". The bracelet takes 64 copper chain links to craft. When the copper chainmail bracelet is worn in a bracelet slot, getting struck by lightning will apply Instant Health III for a tick and Speed III for 5 seconds. Copper chainmail bracelets can be crushed into unfinished copper projects containing 64 chain links. gal
