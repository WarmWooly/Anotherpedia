Copper Chainmail Gloves (Not Lazy Chainmail)

Copper chainmail gloves are a hand curio in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail that has a chance to summon lightning when attacking mobs. Copper chainmail gloves can be created through maille crafting with the settings "Copper Gloves". The gloves take 192 copper chain links to craft. When copper chainmail gloves are worn in a hands slot, the wearer has a 10% chance to summon lightning when hitting a mob. There is a 2% chance that 10 bolts are summoned instead of 1. The chance will increase depending on the number of copper chainmail armor pieces (helmet, chestplate, leggings, and boots) and/or copper chain cover-upgraded pieces the wearer has equipped.
