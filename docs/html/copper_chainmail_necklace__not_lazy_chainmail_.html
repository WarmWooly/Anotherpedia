Copper Chainmail Necklace (Not Lazy Chainmail)

The copper chainmail necklace is a necklace curio in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail that has a chance to summon lightning when the wearer is hit. The copper chainmail necklace can be created through maille crafting with the settings "Copper Necklace". The necklace takes 128 copper chain links to craft. When the copper chainmail necklace is worn in a necklace slot, the wearer has a 10% chance to summon lightning when hit. There is a 2% chance that 10 bolts are summoned instead of 1. The chance will increase depending on the number of copper chainmail armor pieces (helmet, chestplate, leggings, and boots) and/or copper chain cover-upgraded pieces the wearer has equipped.
