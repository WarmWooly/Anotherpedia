Copper Tool Chain (Not Lazy Chainmail)

The copper tool chain is an item in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail used for crafting various tools and weapons. The copper tool chain can be created through maille crafting with the settings "Copper Tool Chain". The tool chain takes 192 copper chain links to craft. Copper tool chains can be obtained when a Crusher crushes a copper whip, copper flail, copper lasso, or copper nunchucks. Copper tool chains are used in the following crafting recipes:
Copper Flail - 1 copper tool chain, 1 block of copper chains, and 2 sticks (place the sticks in the middle and bottom left slots, the tool chain in the top right slot, and the block in the center right slot).
Copper Lasso - 1 copper tool chain and 2 copper chains (place the tool chain in the top right corner and the chains in the middle and bottom right corner).
Copper Nunchucks - 1 copper tool chain, 4 copper nuggets, and 2 sticks (nuggets go in all four corners, the sticks to the left and right, and the tool chain on the bottom).
Copper Rope x4 - 1 copper tool chain, 1 copper chain, and 1 iron ingot (place the tool chain in the middle, chain below, and iron above).
Copper Whip - 1 copper tool chain, 1 leather, and 1 stick (place the leather in the middle, the stick in the bottom left, and the tool chain in the top right). Copper tool chains can be crushed into unfinished copper projects containing 192 chain links. gal
