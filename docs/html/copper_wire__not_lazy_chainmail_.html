Copper Wire (Not Lazy Chainmail)

The copper wire is an item in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail used to be wound into a copper coil. The bulk alternative to the copper wire is the copper spool. Copper wire can be crafted with 8 copper nuggets (place in a circle). Novice-level chainmaillers have a 22.22% chance to trade 2 emeralds for 1 copper wire. The chances to find a copper wire in chests are the following:
In a winding jig ruins, a chest has an 50.16% chance to contain a copper wire.
In a chainmailler home, a chest has a 51.77% chance to contain a copper wire. Copper wire can be used on a winding jig or in an auto winder to be turned into a copper coil.
