Creatology Pumpkin Plush

The Creatology Pumpkin Plush is a stuffed toy pumpkin produced by Creatology for Halloween. It is an orange, 6-segmented pumpkin with a purple stem, black "carved" eyes and mouth, and black legs. It is 7 in. wide and 4.5 in. tall, and its SKU number is 10807999. It is a seasonal product that can be purchased at Michaels stores or online.
