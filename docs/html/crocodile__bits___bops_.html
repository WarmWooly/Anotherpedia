Crocodile (Bits & Bops)

The crocodile is a pink crocodile in Bits & Bops, appearing within the minigame/mixtapes Sweet Tooth, Jungle Mixtape, and Final Mixtape. They are fed by the keeper. The crocodile has its light pink underbelly transition to a darker maroon on top. They have three-toed feet, a long tail, and multiple pronounced scutes along its back. The crocodile feeds on cakes and cupcakes. In the Jungle Mixtape, the crocodile is yellow with a pineapple body, and they feed on watermelon and berries.
