Cuckoo clock (Bits & Bops)

The cuckoo clock is an optional minigame in the game corner in Bits & Bops. The cuckoo clock is unlocked after the Jungle Mixtape is completed, where the player tries to keep the time. After resetting the clock by holding Button 1, the player must press Button 1 when the pendulum is to the left to progress. Every 60 presses, a fake canary will pop out. The minigame ends once the player messes up, with no limit to how long the player can go for. If the player makes it to 60 or more seconds, they will be awarded the pin Timekeeper.
