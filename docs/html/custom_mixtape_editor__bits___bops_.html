Custom mixtape editor (Bits & Bops)

The custom mixtape editor is a work in progress level editor within Bits & Bops to make custom mixtapes, currently in pre-alpha <>. It is available in the demo release on Steam by pressing Ctrl + E on the credits. It includes all minigames and their mixtape variations, with most animations from minigames/mixtapes preserved. It also provides rudimentary visual effects in the form of flashing/fading colors on-screen. Mods can allow players to implement custom assets to their mixtapes.
