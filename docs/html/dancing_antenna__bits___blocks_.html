Dancing Antenna (Bits & Blocks)

The dancing antenna is a head curio in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that applies a buff while sneaking. The dancing antenna can be crafted with 1 B-Bot and the Fly Girls and 1 redstone torch (shapeless). When the dancing antenna is worn in a head slot, sneaking will apply Speed II. The dancing antenna is used in the following crafting recipes:
Windblown Dancing Antenna - 1 dancing antenna and 1 Sky Mixtape (shapeless).
