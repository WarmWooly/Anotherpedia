Dejavi (7thorn)

Dejavi (/ˈdɛd͡ʒəvi/ DEH-juh-vi) is a flow worm OC created by 7thorn in 2026. Her design is based on voidpets and Animal Jam. Dejavi has a black body with dark gray limbs/face and white eyes/mouth. She also has a small white star above her right eye. On her back are two smooth black tentacles with gray ends. Dejavi's skin is soft and ash-like, but the tentacles are hard and nearly indestructible. Her body temperature is 17925 °C and she can only eat "void matter". She is able to increase in length indefinitely, adding more legs to compensate.

Her name is a play on déjà vu. She really likes the holiday Easter and is unable to draw. She knows Amharic while living in Ethiopia. Dejavi is shown to know Blaze.
