Dejavi (7thorn)

Dejavi (/ˈdɛd͡ʒəvi/ DEH-juh-vi) is a flow worm OC created by 7thorn in 2026. Her design is based on voidpets and Animal Jam. Dejavi has a black body with dark gray limbs/face and white eyes/mouth. She also has a small white star above her right eye. On her back are two smooth black tentacles with gray ends. She can increase in length indefinitely, adding more legs to compensate.

Dejavi's skin is soft and ash-like, but the tentacles are hard and nearly indestructible. Her body temperature is 17925 °C and she can only eat "void matter". Her body is comprised of blood, tar, pitch, magma, and "phantom goop". Her insides are also under high pressure. She is both ageless and approximately born on June 28, 1910 BCE, making her <> years old. She is apothiosexual.

Her name is a play on déjà vu. She really likes the holiday Easter and is unable to draw. She knows Amharic while living in Ethiopia. When bored, Dejavi stands upright and makes a loud sound for hours while her face distorts into a vortex. Her small size prevents her from becoming a surgeon. Dejavi is shown to know Blaze.
