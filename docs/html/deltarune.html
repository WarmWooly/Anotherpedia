Deltarune

{{iDeltarune}} (also written as {{ideltarune}} and {{iDELTARUNE}}) is an indie episodic RPG developed by Toby Fox. It was first made available through the release of Chapter 1 in 2018, with the latest chapter being Chapter 5 <>. The game is derived from Toby's 2015 Undertale, sharing characters and some mechanics, with Deltarune being an anagram of Undertale.

The game features a party lead by the human teen Kris, usually alongside their friends, including a monster classmate Susie and the darkner Ralsei, to seal Dark Fountains in Dark Worlds. The combat uses a turn-based system with dodging being a culmination of bullet hell segments and minigames. Deltarune also takes inspiration from other video games for some of its gameplay segments, such as Punch-Out!! and The Legend of Zelda.
