Disco (Bits & Bops)

Disco is an anthropomorphic cat in Bits & Bops, appearing in the lounge. She comments on various souvenirs about swiping them. She will comment on the commemorative coin (President Bird), the coin chest (Snakedown), and the black oil lamp (Fire Mixtape). Notably, her dialogue mentions that the commemorative coin is made of plastic. She was a backer reward purchased by the content creator Jenny Chongo. Disco is a slate blue human-shaped cat with long arms and legs alongside a tail. She has four fingers and three toes, with lobes around the side of her head. Her ears point upward, with a pair of gold rings attached to her left ear. Her face has a smooth W-shaped mouth, a dark nose, and yellow eyes. She also has dark markings on her face, with two acting as eyebrows. At the end of her tail are two dark stripes. Disco wears a red shirt with a very long collar and brown pants with dark markings on the legs.
