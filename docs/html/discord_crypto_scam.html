Discord crypto scam

The Discord crypto scam is a spray-and-pray imposter scam over Discord where a hijacked account pings @everyone with images of a crypto casino. The images show a fake announcement of a partnership with a crypto casino, with a free code promising hundreds to thousands in crypto. The scam 'impersonates' the hijacked user, relying on victims to believe that the user is trying to share the free money offered by the crypto casino. The main benefit of hijacking an account over creating a bot account is that the account is already trusted and likely on multiple servers and/or with multiple friends that can be immediately accessed. The initial hijacking tends to be a trojan infostealer that bypasses the normal password/multi-factor authentication, with a message programmatically sent on every available channel with the identical ping and images.
