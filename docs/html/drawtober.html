Drawtober

Drawtober is an annual internet challenge during October where participants complete artworks based on a list of prompts over the month, usually with a prompt a day (for 31 prompts in total). The list may be randomly generated or provided by a community beforehand. Depending on the community, they may also have themed prompts, including specific topics or characters. A common theme is Halloween, being a large holiday associated with October.
