Drum kit

A drum kit or drum set (informally drums) is an arrangement of percussion instruments played by one person including drums and one or more cymbals. They may also include other mounted percussion instruments, with the musician using drumsticks and other mallets alongside pedals to play the drum kit. The musician either sits on a stool or stands surrounded by the instruments while playing. The musician who plays a drum kit is known as a drummer.
