Drum stool

For the seat shaped like a drum, see drum-shaped stool.
A drum stool, drum chair, or drum throne is a stool that is used by a drummer playing a drum kit. Drum stools are designed to support the drummer while being unobstructive to interacting with any of the instruments attached to the drum kit. The most common design for a drum stool is a fabric seat placed on an adjustable metal rod with a tripod underneath for stability.
