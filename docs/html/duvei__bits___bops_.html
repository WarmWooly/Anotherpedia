Duvei (Bits & Bops)

Duvei is an anthropomorphic moth in Bits & Bops, appearing in the lounge. He talks about his experience as a singer and asks the player if they also like attention. He will comment on the black oil lamp (Fire Mixtape) souvenir, being drawn to the light. He was a backer reward purchased by the content creator AuraPuffs. Duvei is a small humanoid moth with short arms and legs. He has a yellow body with pink arms, legs, and collar. He has three fingers and two toes, with a pair of wings on his back and large, orange antennae. His body has darker lines segmenting it. Duvei has two lobes on his head like hair, with large black eyes with white pupils and a mouth below. His forewings are pink with a yellow stripe down the middle, while the hindwings are pink with yellow tips.
