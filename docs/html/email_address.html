Email address

An email address is a unique string that is used to represent a digital mailbox for emails. Email addresses are usually constructed of an identifier (also called the username or local-part) and a domain name. For example, writer@anotherpedia.com would have the identifier writer over the domain anotherpedia.com. Email addresses may represent a single person, a company or organization, or an automated account.
