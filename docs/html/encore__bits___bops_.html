Encore (Bits & Bops)

Encore is the fourth and last cartridge in the game corner in Bits & Bops, following the Fire Mixtape. The player is a member of an audience, trying to get them all to applaud. The player controls a member of the audience, pressing Button 1 to clap. The player must slowly clap, bringing more of the audience in as they get faster. Once everyone is applauding, a scene from a minigame will be shown. If the player sees all minigames (Flipper Snapper, Rock, Paper, Showdown!, Meet & Tweet, Firework Festival, Molecano, President Bird, Snakedown, and Octeaparty), then they are awarded the pin Bravo!. Attempting the cartridge unlocks the Final Mixtape.
