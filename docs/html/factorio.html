Factorio

{{iFactorio}} is a construction and management simulation video game fully released in 2020 by Wube Software. The game features an engineer that has crash landed on the alien planet Nauvis, with the end goal to build a rocket silo to escape. Players are expected to automate resource harvesting and production to scale up their base, fend off aliens, and build and launch a rocket. Factorio also has the expansion Space Age, released in 2024, which adds additional planets with their own unique buildings and challenges alongside a new end goal for the game.
