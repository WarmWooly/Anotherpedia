Farakey (Bits & Bops)

Farakey is an anthropomorphic bird in Bits & Bops, appearing in the lounge. He is shown to know Poodley and Jazz. He can also ask the player if they like to get Perfects, with Farakey personally being determined to score a Perfect when he plays. He was a backer reward purchased by the content creator Kevin Fagaragan. Farakey is a blue humanoid bird with long arms and legs. He has four fingers, with skinny, yellow legs like a bird. He has a round head with a cream color along the bottom connecting to a yellow beak. He also has dot eyes with black eyebrows and blush lines. Farakey wears a cream shirt with a red vest and black bottom wear, alongside a pair of red and cream shoes and black and yellow headphones. He holds a squeeze toy of Bit, which is a nod to Clap Trap from Rhythm Heaven.
