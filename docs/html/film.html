Film

For other terms relating to film, see film (diambiguation).
"Movie" and "motion picture" redirect here. For other terms, see movie (disambiguation) and motion picture (disambiguation).
A film, movie, or motion picture is art made up of many images which are played rapidly in order to create the illusion of motion, usually with accompanying sound, to tell a story. They can be created through filming on a camera, creating an animation, or any other method that produces a series of frames or moving images. The process of making a film is known as filmmaking. Films are shown within theaters and on television.
