Final Mixtape (Bits & Bops)

Fire Mixtape is the fifth and last mixtape within Bits & Bops, containing all minigames in the game. Only one change is made, where the last ball the seal bounces in Flipper Snapper is a party ball containing confetti. The minigames appear in the following order: ::Flipper Snapper|Flow Worms|Sweet Tooth|Steady Bears|President Bird|B-Bot and the Fly Girls|Firework Festival|Pantry Parade|Molecano|Snakedown|Globe Trotters|Rock, Paper, Showdown!|Hammer Time!|Octeaparty|Meet & Tweet|Pop-Up Kitchen|Flipper Snapper::Some of the minigames (most notably Octeaparty) are faster than their standalone counterparts to match the song.
