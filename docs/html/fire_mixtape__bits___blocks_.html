Fire Mixtape (Bits & Blocks)

The Fire Mixtape is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks based on the mixtape of the same name. It can be displayed on a mixtape stand. The Fire Mixtape can be crafted with 1 mixtape case, 1 President Bird, 1 Snakedown, 1 Octeaparty, and 1 Globe Trotters (shapeless). The Fire Mixtape is used in the following crafting recipes:
Blazing Trotting Antennae - 1 Fire Mixtape, 1 trotting antennae, and 1 blaze rod (shapeless).
Burnt Small Tie - 1 Fire Mixtape and 1 small tie (shapeless).
Final Mixtape - 1 Fire Mixtape, 1 Jungle Mixtape, 1 Sky Mixtape, and 1 Ocean Mixtape (shapeless).
Silhouetted Tentacles - 1 Fire Mixtape and 1 tentacles (shapeless).
Tempered Pot Lid - 1 Fire Mixtape and 1 pot lid (shapeless).
