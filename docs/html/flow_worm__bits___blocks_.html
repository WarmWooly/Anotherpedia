Flow Worm (Bits & Blocks)

For the item, see Flow Worms ({{aiBits & Blocks).}}
A flow worm is a passive mob in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that can be positioned and bounced on, directly based on the fictional species. They have multiple variants and will glow at night or in the dark. Flow worms can be spawned when a hollow dirt is placed. Each hollow dirt will only spawn a single flow worm. The flow worm will be one of eight variants based on the colors and expressions of flow worms in-game, with an equal probability for each variant. The /summon command can be used to spawn flow worms:/summon bits_and_blocks:flow_worm This command will spawn varients at their normal rates. Using a flow worm spawn egg will also spawn a flow worm with its variants at a normal rate. Flow worms will stay motionless, only randomly turning around and floating in water. When the light level is 5 or less, or between the times 13000 and 23000 (nighttime), the flow worm will visibly glow. The color it glows is tied to its variant.

Interacing with a flow worm above their head will cause them to bop the player, bouncing them upwards. The sound they make is directly tied to their variant.

Flow worms can be attached to leads, allowing them to be moved. Flow worms will jump up 1-block ledges while pulled by a lead. Flow worms are considered arthropods and take increased damage from Bane of Arthropods Flow worms can be altered, changing their behavior. Using items on them does not consume the item. If the player sneaks while interacting with them, then the flow worm will face their direction. The following items can be used, changing the flow worm:;;The Sky Mixtape will change the flow worm to its mixtape appearance, with new colors, wings, and a halo (can be reversed with Flow Worms).|Glowstone will make the flow worm always glow, and tinted glass will make the flow worm never glow (can be reversed with a Clock).|A chain will lock the rotation of the flow worm and stop it from swimming (can be reversed with Shears).;; The variant of the flow worm can be directly selected using a name tag, in the form of NPC 1, NPC 2, NPC 3, NPC 4, NPC 5, NPC 6, Player, and NPC 7 for the flow worms as seen along the ground in-game. Slow can also be used to get the appearance of the slow-moving flow worm.

Anno will make the flow worm look like Anno, the mascot of Anotherpedia (with the mod and the site both made by Warm_Wooly). Anno can't glow and does not have a mixtape version.

Blaze and Romi will make flow worms look like Inverterate's Blaze and Romi. They do not have mixtape versions. GT will make the flow worms look like globe trotters, also suggested by Inverterate.
