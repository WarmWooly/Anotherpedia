Flow worms (fictional species)

Flow worms (also called worms) are a ficitional species of bug within the rhythm game Bits & Bops, appearing within the minigame/mixtapes Flow Worms, Sky Mixtape, and Final Mixtape. They are seen bouncing other flow worms along their heads to move them along. For more on coloration and pose, see flow worm distinction.
Flow worms are caterpillar-like, with tall, rounded bodies and a large head. They have six legs and a pair of antennae. Flow worms have nearly-black dot eyes and a smooth W-shaped mouth. Their bodies are one color, while their limbs, antennae, and the area around their mouth are another.

Flow worms are seen glowing in the dark like glowworms, while having bright coloration similar to gummy worms. Most flow worms are green, with a few (notably the player) red and magenta. When glowing, green flow worms are vibrant green, blue, and/or yellow. Flow worms curl up into balls when bouncing along other worms, which is how they dance together. They can burrow into the ground like worms. While not shown in-game, they have also been suggested to do the worm.

During the Sky Mixtape, the NPC flow worms have a pair of two-lobed wings on their backs and a halo over their heads. These flow worms are light blue and white, with the player flow worm being golden. While glowing, the blue flow worms can also be a light yellow. The design of flow worms changed throughout the development of Bits & Bops. Originally, as seen in the art book, concepts of a group of isopods bouncing others along were used while developing the minigame. It would later iterate to glowing worm-like characters. An early development image showed various characteristics that were changed between the concept and final designs, notably a lack of legs and a three-color body during development. The glowing sprites for flow worms were created first, with a daytime palette created afterwards to complement the song with a flashy transition during the breakdown to the glowing palette. Flow worms appear in the form of wall lamps in Momento. They are under the lighting tab for Objects, and come in green, blue, and purple.
