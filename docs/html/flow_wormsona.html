Flow wormsona

A flow wormsona or wormsona is a sona of a flow worm from Bits & Bops. They are predominately made by furries, with colors and body design of the flow worm changed to fit the OC or fursona that is being turned into a flow worm. They are especially prevalent on Bluesky, and were most popular during early 2026.
