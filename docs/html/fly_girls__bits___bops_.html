Fly Girls (Bits & Bops)

The Fly Girls are a pair of backup dancers in Bits & Bops, appearing within the minigame/mixtapes B-Bot and the Fly Girls, Sky Mixtape, and Final Mixtape. One of them also appears on the title screen. They dance alongside B-Bot, and are shown tweaking B-Bot on the postcard photo for "Try Again" in B-Bot and the Fly Girls. Both of the Fly Girls have the same appearance, with dark skin, dark purple hair, and red lipstick. They wear short white T-shirts, dark purple baggy pants, yellow baseball caps, yellow and white shoes, and yellow wristbands. In the Sky Mixtape, they have gray shirts, dark blue hair and pants, red hats and wristbands, and red and gray shoes.
