Forging Hammer (Bits & Blocks)

The forging hammer is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that repairs the durability of the currently held item passively. The forging hammer can be crafted with 1 Blacksmith, 1 iron ingot, and 1 stick (shapeless). While the forging hammer is held in the inventory, it will repair 10 durability from the item in the main hand every 5 seconds. It will not exceed the maximum durability of the item.
