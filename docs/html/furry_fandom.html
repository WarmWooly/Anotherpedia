Furry fandom

The furry fandom is a fandom comprised by people with interest in anthropomorphic animal characters. Members of the fandom are known as furries. The fandom spans in-person events (notably furry conventions) and over the Internet. Members generally adopt a fursona, a fictional representation of themselves as an anthropomorphic animal, and may also wear a costume (known as a fursuit) based on it.
