Fursona

A fursona (a combination of fur and -sona) is a persona of an anthropomorphic animal used within the furry fandom. While usually referring to mammals, a fursona can be of any animal or fictional creature. The most common traits of fursonas are being humanoid in shape with animalistic features, such as a muzzle and/or tail. Fursonas are used within art, roleplay, and the design of fursuits.
