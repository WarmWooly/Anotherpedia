Globe Trotters (Bits & Blocks)

Globe Trotters is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks based on the minigame of the same name. It can be displayed on a minigame stand. Globe Trotters can be crafted with 1 minigame case, 1 end stone, and 1 netherrack (shapeless). Globe Trotters is used in the following crafting recipes:
Fire Mixtape - 1 Globe Trotters, 1 mixtape case, 1 President Bird, 1 Snakedown, and 1 Octeaparty (shapeless).
Tentacles - 1 Globe Trotters and 2 shulker shells (shapeless).
