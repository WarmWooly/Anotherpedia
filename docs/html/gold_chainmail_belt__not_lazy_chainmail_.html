Gold Chainmail Belt (Not Lazy Chainmail)

The gold chainmail belt is a belt curio in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail that passively applies Speed. The gold chainmail belt can be created through maille crafting with the settings "Gold Belt". The belt takes 128 gold chain links to craft. When the gold chainmail belt is worn in a belt slot, the wearer will gain Speed. The Speed will increase depending on the number of gold chainmail armor pieces (helmet, chestplate, leggings, and boots) and/or gold chain cover-upgraded pieces the wearer has equipped. With 2 armor pieces equipped, the effect given is Speed II, and with all 4 armor pieces equipped, the effect given is Speed III. Gold chainmail belts can be crushed into unfinished gold projects containing 128 chain links. gal
