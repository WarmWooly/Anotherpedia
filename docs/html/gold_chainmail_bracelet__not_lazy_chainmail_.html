Gold Chainmail Bracelet (Not Lazy Chainmail)

The gold chainmail bracelet is a bracelet curio in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail that prevents the wearer from getting Blindness, Darkness, and Slowness. The gold chainmail bracelet can be created through maille crafting with the settings "Gold Bracelet". The bracelet takes 64 gold chain links to craft. Gold chainmail bracelets have a 3.85% chance of being found in suspicious sand within chainmail ruins. When the gold chainmail bracelet is worn in a bracelet slot, it will prevent the wearer from getting the effects Blindness, Darkness, and Slowness until removed. If the effects Blindness, Darkness, and/or Slowness are currently applied when equipping the gold chainmail bracelet, they are removed. Gold chainmail bracelets can be crushed into unfinished gold projects containing 64 chain links. gal
