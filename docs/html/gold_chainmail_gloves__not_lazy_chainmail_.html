Gold Chainmail Gloves (Not Lazy Chainmail)

Gold chainmail gloves are a hand curio in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail that applies Speed III when attacking mobs. Gold chainmail gloves can be created through maille crafting with the settings "Gold Gloves". The gloves take 192 gold chain links to craft. When gold chainmail gloves are worn in a hand slot, they will apply Speed III for 5 seconds to the wearer after attacking a mob. Gold chainmail gloves can be crushed into unfinished gold projects containing 192 chain links. gal
