Gold Chainmail Necklace (Not Lazy Chainmail)

The gold chainmail necklace is a necklace curio in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail that applies Haste III when the wearer is hit. The gold chainmail necklace can be created through maille crafting with the settings "Gold Necklace". The necklace takes 128 gold chain links to craft. Gold chainmail necklaces have a 3.85% chance of being found in suspicious sand within chainmail ruins. When the gold chainmail necklace is worn in a necklace slot, the wearer gains Haste III for 15 seconds when hit. Gold chainmail necklaces can be crushed into unfinished gold projects containing 128 chain links. gal
