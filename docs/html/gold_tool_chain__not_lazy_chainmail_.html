Gold Tool Chain (Not Lazy Chainmail)

The gold tool chain is an item in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail used for crafting various tools and weapons. The gold tool chain can be created through maille crafting with the settings "Gold Tool Chain". The tool chain takes 192 gold chain links to craft. Gold tool chains can be obtained when a Crusher crushes a gold whip, gold flail, gold lasso, or gold nunchucks. Gold tool chains are used in the following crafting recipes:
Gold Flail - 1 gold tool chain, 1 block of gold chains, and 2 sticks (place the sticks in the middle and bottom left slots, the tool chain in the top right slot, and the block in the center right slot).
Gold Lasso - 1 gold tool chain and 2 gold chains (place the tool chain in the top right corner and the chains in the middle and bottom right corner).
Gold Nunchucks - 1 gold tool chain, 4 gold nuggets, and 2 sticks (nuggets go in all four corners, the sticks to the left and right, and the tool chain on the bottom).
Gold Rope x4 - 1 gold tool chain, 1 gold chain, and 1 iron ingot (place the tool chain in the middle, chain below, and iron above).
Gold Whip - 1 gold tool chain, 1 leather, and 1 stick (place the leather in the middle, the stick in the bottom left, and the tool chain in the top right). Gold tool chains can be crushed into unfinished gold projects containing 192 chain links. gal
