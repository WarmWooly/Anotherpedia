Hache (The After Game)

Hache (formerly The After Game) is a Spanish-speaking YouTuber. The channel focuses on making reviews and analyses, especially for Silent Hill and Cronos: The New Dawn. His videos are primarily gameplay, with a facecam he uses while playing. He utilizes generative AI for his thumbnails. He also has a second channel, HacheAfterGame.
