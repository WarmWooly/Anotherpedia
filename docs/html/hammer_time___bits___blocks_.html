Hammer Time! (Bits & Blocks)

Hammer Time! is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks based on the minigame of the same name. It can be displayed on a minigame stand. Hammer Time! can be crafted with 1 minigame case, 1 iron ingot, and 1 wooden planks (shapeless). Hammer Time! is used in the following crafting recipes:
Instruction Sheet - 1 Hammer Time! and 1 paper (shapeless).
Ocean Mixtape - 1 Hammer Time!, 1 mixtape case, 1 Pop-Up Kitchen, 1 Firework Festival, and 1 Molecano (shapeless).
