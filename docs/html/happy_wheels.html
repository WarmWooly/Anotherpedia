Happy Wheels

{{iHappy Wheels}} is a 2010 physics platformer browser game designed by Jim Bronacci and published by Fancy Force. It features various characters who ride vehicles, including bikes, a pogo stick, a Segway, and Santa's sleigh powered by elves. Happy Wheels is well-known for its user-generated levels and graphic content caused by characters being mamed and killed in levels. It was especially popular on YouTube in the early-2010s due to its user creations, largely spread by PewDiePie, Jacksepticeye, and Markiplier.
