Hellish Glass (Not Lazy Chainmail)

Hellish glass is a translucent block in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail, sharing the same blast resistance with obsidian. Any tool can be used to mine hellish glass, but the tool must have Silk Touch to collect the hellish glass. Hellish sand can be smelted in a furnace to produce 1 hellish glass, alongside 1 experience. A Cobblestone Generator with a haunt'n'crush'n'smeltifier will produce hellish glass. Haunt'n'crush'n'smeltifiers take 32x as long to generate one hellish glass compared to one cobblestone. Hellish glass is used in the following crafting recipes:
Hellish Glass Pane x16 - 6 hellish glass (fill the middle and bottom rows). Hellish glass is used in the following melding recipes:
Hellish Leather Padding - 2 hellish glass and 1 leather padding
