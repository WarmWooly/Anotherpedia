Hippo (Inverterate)

Hippo is a bambisona OC created by Inverterate in 2023. Hippo has a polygonal body composed of three lust-red cylinders for a lower body, a black hemisphere for a torso, a combination of a normal black sphere and a heavily stretched one for the hat, and a silent yellow sphere for a head. He is optionally seen depicted with hands of the same color as his head, either seen as single spheres or as a flattened sphere with custom objects attached.

Hippo has a rude personality, not showing any support towards others as he believes that being a jerk makes him cool, along with having a sailor mouth. Hippo has a heavily bitcrushed voice, sometimes becoming a noise similar to that of the Wii crash sound. Hippo has, on multiple occasions, brought others to his own personal pocket dimension in order to "free them from their 2nd dimensional prison".

Hippo knows about the fourth wall, and often acknowledges it to either confuse people or just to be funny. Hippo gets INCREDIBLY mad whenever he hears any mention of gen alpha/beta slang, usually resulting in him harming whoever said it. Hippo is just straight up stupid. Hippo is on bad terms with Blaze and Romi, and is also implied to know about Anno.
