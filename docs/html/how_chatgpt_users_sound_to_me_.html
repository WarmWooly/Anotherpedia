How ChatGPT users sound to me:

"How ChatGPT users sound to me:" is a series of short form comedy videos posted by Pineapple Honeydew on TikTok and YouTube Shorts satirizing people who use AI chatbots such as ChatGPT. Each video is comprised of multiple questions that Pineapple Honeydew asks to "ChatBBT", with the questions often being simplistic or obvious (such as asking where her glasses are while they are on her head). She is usually seen wearing two pairs of glasses, looking downwards at her phone while it records her. Some of the videos have a simple plot throughout, like her asking how to cook toast and later asking if her house is on fire.
