Andrew Wagenheim, known online as Huggbees and HuggbeesTV, is an American YouTuber and former podcaster and Twitch streamer. He was born on November 10, 1991, making him <> years old. His username originated from a gag in the cartoon Freakazoid! Huggbees had hosted The Official Podcast from 2016 as a founding member until his departure in 2026.

On YouTube, he is well-known for his series How It's Actually Made, where he redubs the documentary series How It's Made. He also creates other redubs and videos on an assortment of topics, including Wikipedia articles, comic books, television shows, films, video games, and historical events that he's interested in. His most consistent trait is wearing a pair of sunglasses, which he wears in his YouTube videos and on The Official Podcast. He has a second channel, Huggbees: Gamer Mode, for gameplay videos, and a former VOD channel Huggbees Full Streams, before it was terminated. The name of his main channel was formerly TheLoneN00b.
