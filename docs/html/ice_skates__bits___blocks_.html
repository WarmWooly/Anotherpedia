Ice Skates (Bits & Blocks)

Ice skates are a feet curio in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that increases speed and movement control on ice. The ice skates can be crafted with 1 Steady Bears, 1 iron ingot, 1 leather boots, and 1 light blue dye (shapeless). When the ice skates are worn in a feet slot, the player will accelerate faster on ice, packed ice, blue ice, and frosted ice. Sneaking on ice will cause the player to decelerate quickly. Ice skates are used in the following crafting recipes:
Rainbow Skates - 1 ice skates and 1 Sky Mixtape (shapeless).
