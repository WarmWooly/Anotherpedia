Instruction Sheet (Bits & Blocks)

The instruction sheet is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that allows the player to continue placing blocks in the direction they are facing. The instruction sheet can be crafted with 1 Hammer Time! and 1 paper (shapeless). When the instruction sheet is used on a block, it will attempt to place another in the direction the player is facing. If the player does not have the same block in their inventory or the direction is already filled with a block, nothing will happen. It is most useful for bridging as the player does not need to sneak over the edge to place more blocks. The instruction sheet is used in the following crafting recipes:
Blueprint - 1 instruction sheet, 1 Ocean Mixtape, and 1 blue dye (shapeless).
