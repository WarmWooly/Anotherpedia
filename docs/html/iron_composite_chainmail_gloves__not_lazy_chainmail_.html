Iron Composite Chainmail Gloves (Not Lazy Chainmail)

Iron composite chainmail gloves are a hand curio in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail that causes the wearer to ride attacked mobs while passively dealing damage to mobs the player is riding. Iron composite chainmail gloves can be created through maille crafting with the settings "Iron Composite Gloves". The gloves take 192 iron composite chain links to craft. When iron composite chainmail gloves are worn in a hand slot, they will cause the wearer to ride the hit mob. While wearing, any mob the wearer is riding will take constant damage, with the damage increasing based on the number of iron composite chainmail armor pieces (helmet, chestplate, leggings, and boots) and/or iron composite chain cover-upgraded pieces the wearer has equipped.
