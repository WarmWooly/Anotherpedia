Iron Composite Chainmail Necklace (Not Lazy Chainmail)

The iron composite chainmail necklace is a necklace curio in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail that applies Strength when the wearer is hit. The iron composite chainmail necklace can be created through maille crafting with the settings "Iron Composite Necklace". The necklace takes 128 iron composite chain links to craft. When the iron composite chainmail necklace is worn in a necklace slot, the wearer will get Stength II for 10 seconds when hit. The Strength will increase with the number of iron composite chainmail armor pieces (helmet, chestplate, leggings, and boots) and/or iron composite chain cover-upgraded pieces the wearer has equipped. With 2 armor pieces equipped, the effect given is Strength III, and with all 4 armor pieces equipped, the effect given is Strength IV. Iron composite chainmail necklaces can be crushed into unfinished iron composite projects containing 128 chain links. gal
