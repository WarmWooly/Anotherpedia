Iron Composite Wire (Not Lazy Chainmail)

The iron composite wire is an item in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail used to be wound into an iron composite coil. The bulk alternative to the iron composite wire is the iron composite spool. Iron composite wire can be crafted with 8 iron composite nuggets (place in a circle). Master-level chainmaillers have a 100% chance to trade 10 emeralds for 1 iron composite wire. Iron composite wire can be used on a winding jig or in an auto winder to be turned into an iron composite coil.
