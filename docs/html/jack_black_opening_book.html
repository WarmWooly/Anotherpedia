Jack Black opening book

Jack Black opening book is a meme of Jack Black opening up a book. The book then shines a golden light on Black's face, being in shock and awe at its contents. It comes from the ninth episode of Jablinski Games season 1, titled "Ninja responded..." 7 minutes into the episode, Black ponders what the first game was, grabbing a book labeled "Tutankhamun" and opening it. Afterwards, he plays a game of senet as mentioned in the book.
