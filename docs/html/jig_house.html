Jig House

{{iJig House}} is a rhythm game made for the 2019 Global Game Jam. It features three short minigames with different rhythm patterns, heavily inspired by Rhythm Heaven. Jig House would later be developed into Bits & Bops, with two of the minigames being transferred over. It was created by Even Andrews, with music by Julian Sanchez and Asahi Takahashi, and art by Jacinta Joe, Liz Bennett, and Rose Hammer. There are three minigames in Jig House, where the player must press Space to hit the beat. Each minigame features a short tutorial beforehand, where the player can see where the inputs are relative to the beat, alongside any audio cues. The first minigame features a player-controlled yellow canary speaking with a brown and blue wren, with the wren having two speaking patterns. The first has the wren chirp twice, with the player immediately responding on the following beat. The second has the wren chirp three times, with the player waiting a beat before responding. The song and level concept would be reused in Bits & Bops as the minigame Meet & Tweet. The second minigame features a player-controlled maid who must cut various foods that roll across the kitchen counter. Three foods can appear: a green cabbage, which slowly rolls for three beats; a red cabbage, which has a bell ring twice before rolling for a beat; and a head of lettuce, which rolls for four beats. The third minigame features a player-controlled ant alongside others who are carrying grapes. The player marches, inputting every beat. When a whistle is blown twice, the player waits half a beat before pressing twice, half a beat apart. The camera would also sometimes zoom out into a wide shot of all the ants crossing. The concept would be reworked in Bits & Bops as the minigame Pantry Parade.
