Jungle Mixtape (Bits & Blocks)

The Jungle Mixtape is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks based on the mixtape of the same name. It can be displayed on a mixtape stand. The Jungle Mixtape can be crafted with 1 mixtape case, 1 Flipper Snapper, 1 Sweet Tooth, 1 Rock, Paper, Showdown!, and 1 Pantry Parade (shapeless). The Jungle Mixtape is used in the following crafting recipes:
Exotic Parading Legs - 1 Jungle Mixtape, 1 parading legs, and 1 leaves (shapeless).
Final Mixtape - 1 Jungle Mixtape, 1 Sky Mixtape, 1 Ocean Mixtape, and 1 Fire Mixtape (shapeless).
Mangrove Apple - 1 Jungle Mixtape, 1 beach ball, and 1 apple (shapeless).
Ripe Tooth Necklace - 1 Jungle Mixtape and 1 tooth necklace (shapeless).
Wild Rock Paper Scissors - 1 Jungle Mixtape and 1 rock paper scissors (shapeless).
