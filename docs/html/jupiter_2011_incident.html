Jupiter 2011 incident

The Jupiter 2011 incident or Odyssey 9 disaster is a fictional NASA space disaster, in which a crew of five (rarely six) are attacked by extraterrestrials near Jupiter or on a moon of Jupiter (notably Callisto or Europa), with only the captain escaping. It would be a meme created November 21, 2026, using generative AI to depict various public figures as astronauts on the supposed "Odyssey 9 mission". Due to the rapid spread of the meme, there are multiple tellings of the Jupiter 2011 incident with conflicting information. The crew would be comprised of CaseOh as the captain, with KreekCraft, Joe Bartolozzi, IShowSpeed, and the fictional Mateo O. Algo in various other roles.

The meme would spread due to its depiction of various influencers in serious roles, with content made around the event treating it as real. Figures depicted within the Jupiter 2011 incident, including KreekCraft and CaseOh, would react to it. Other stories would attempt to be spun off of the meme in the same manner, most notably the Mars 1987 incident with DanTDM, Jacksepticeye, Markiplier, and PewDiePie. The general outline that the meme follows is that a secret or public space mission is planned by NASA, with a space launch in March 2011 of the Odyssey 9. The mission's goal is generally described as either finding extraterrestrial life or inhabiting other worlds in space. In August 2011, the Odyssey 9 would arrive near Jupiter or on one of its moons. Shortly after, a species of flying key-shaped extraterrestrials known as "Kies" would attack the crew, generally being shown killing all but CaseOh. CaseOh would use a rotary machine gun to fend off the Kies before escaping.

Some tellings describe probes and search and rescue operations done after the event to find CaseOh, who is sometimes described as missing and presumed dead until arriving back to Earth in an escape pod. Footage, photographs, and audio recordings would be recovered and released in 2026. The most popular would be a 38-second flight recording of the Kies attacking the crew, with CaseOh firing a machine gun to fend them off.
