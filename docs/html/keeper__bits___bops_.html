Keeper (Bits & Bops)

The keeper is the caretaker for the crocodile in Bits & Bops, appearing within the minigame/mixtapes Sweet Tooth, Jungle Mixtape, and Final Mixtape. The keeper has tanned skin with brown hair with twin braids. She wears a tan hat and shirt, blue shorts and socks, and brown boots. She has a round cart with four wheels to transport food. In the Jungle Mixtape, she has a yellow hat and shirt, green shorts and socks, and purple boots. Her cart is also made out of a different material (similar to a wicker basket).
