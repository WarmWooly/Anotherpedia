LarpTuber

A LarpTuber is a YouTuber who larps as another YouTuber. This is usually done by mimicking the mannerisms, editing, and design elements of a YouTuber to recreate the style of their content, with a focus on a specific time range (such as 2016--2018). LarpTubers tend to have the word "larp" somewhere within their name to distinguish themselves as a LarpTuber. LarpTubers started appearing around early 2026, with creators such as Larpiplier and Technolarp (playing off Markiplier and Technoblade) appearing around then. LarpTubers may be controversial due to their style being constructed by taking the elements of established creators, and in the case of Technoblade, by taking the mannerisms and likeness of a deceased creator.
