Leg Band (Bits & Blocks)

The leg band is a feet curio in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that applies a buff while being near other players. The leg band can be crafted with 1 Three-Legged Race, 1 leather, and 1 orange dye (shapeless). When the leg band is worn in a feet slot, being 8 blocks or less from a player will apply Speed and Haste.
