Lemon

A lemon is an oval-shaped yellow Citrus fruit (technically a berry) with a rind, which grow off of lemon trees. Lemons are well-known for their sourness. Its rind is odorous, which can be used as a zest or in essential oils. The fruit inside contains the seeds, and can be eaten straight or squeezed into lemon juice. Lemonade is a popular drink derived from lemons. Lemon slices may be used to make a dish more sour.
