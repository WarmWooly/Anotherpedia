Lemon loaf

A lemon loaf or lemon bread is a lemon-flavored pound cake baked within a bread pan, matching the shape of a bread loaf. Lemon loaves have a white, lemon-flavored icing covering the top, while lemon breads do not. The loaf tends to be a light brown/soft yellow in color after baking. It is traditionally served as slices. The coffeehouse chain Starbucks sells lemon loaf slices <>.
