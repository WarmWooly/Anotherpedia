Lemon water

Lemon water is a drink made from combining lemon juice or other parts of a lemon with water. A recipe may have a sliced lemon sit in a pitcher or large container of water to allow the lemon's flavor to diffuse into the water. Lemon water is slightly more sour than normal water, with the main factor coming from the ratio of lemon juice to water. Lemon water is usually served cold.
