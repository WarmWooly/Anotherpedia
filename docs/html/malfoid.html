Malfoid

Malfoid (also called Galfoy) is a late-2026 art trend/internet meme of depicting the Harry Potter antagonist Draco Malfoy as a genderbent "foid". Common traits include Malfoid being tsundere, being shipped with Harry Potter, and being depicted in a live action or an anime art style. Fan art of the character would be popular on social media, including DeviantArt, Twitter, Bluesky, and iFunny.

While many depictions of Malfoid are human-made, a large subset of fan works utilize generative AI. The character is also heavily sexualized, despite usually being depicted as a minor. An AI-generated dating sim Pure Blood & Prejudice would be produced for the character during the trend.
