Marchio the Numel is a NSFW Numel Pokésona created in 2025 by ShapeSheeping. Marchio is primarily posted on Bluesky and FurAffinity as the transformed Pokémon form of ShapeSheeping. Marchio is a Numel with a brown body and light brown underbelly, snout, and feet. She also has a short, triangular tail, also with a lighter underside. She has a thick head of hair, extending down to her snout and neck. Marchio's eyes are blue, with a thinner outline compared to normal Numels. The pattern along her hump is smooth and red, with blobby fragments around the covering. She is shown wearing various carpets on her back as clothing, with a hole around her vent. She has been shown wearing a bandana and standing upright in an artist's outfit.

As the transformed version of ShapeSheeping, Marchio's age matches the user. She is non-binary and is generally lax. She enjoys spicy foods and hates bitter foods. She has the moveset Earth Power, Ember, Rest, and Yawn.
