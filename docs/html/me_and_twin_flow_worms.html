me and twin flow worms

me and twin flow worms is a variation on the me and twin meme using a pair of flow worms from Bits & Bops. Specifically, it is a GIF of the NPC 6 and player worms bopping, with the caption "me and twin" in a white text box above. Its earliest appearance is on the Discord server for Bits & Bops, posted by CeloCogumelo on December 10, 2026. It would later become popular within the Bits & Bops community.
