Message chain

Not to be confused with a chain letter.
A message chain is a series of multiple messages that directly build off the previous, generally with several participants. Message chains tend to be iterative, with each corresponding message being based on the prior (such as counting up). These chains may be spontaneous, or may be constructed beforehand. Message chains are most popular digitally, as text messages have little delay and can be easily tracked. Message chains may be broken by inactivity, a message that fails to iterate correctly (i.e. "12" --> "13" --> "14" --> "18"), or another message separate from the chain. Counting channels are a popular form of message chain on Discord, where users make a message chain counting up from 1.
