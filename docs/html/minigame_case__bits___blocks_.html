Minigame Case (Bits & Blocks)

The minigame case is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks used for crafting the minigame items. The minigame case can be crafted with 4 iron nuggets and 1 paper (place the paper in the middle and surround it with iron). Minigame cases are used in the following crafting recipes:
B-Bot and the Fly Girls - 1 minigame case, 1 gold ingot, and 1 redstone comparator (shapeless).
Firework Festival - 1 minigame case and 2 firework rockets (shapeless).
Flipper Snapper - 1 minigame case, 1 lever, and 1 glass (shapeless).
Flow Worms - 1 minigame case, 1 glowstone dust, 1 dirt/coarse dirt/rooted dirt, and 1 moss block/grass block/grass/tall grass/fern/vines (shapeless).
Globe Trotters - 1 minigame case, 1 end stone, and 1 netherrack (shapeless).
Hammer Time! - 1 minigame case, 1 iron ingot, and 1 wooden planks (shapeless).
Meet & Tweet - 1 minigame case, 1 baked potato, and 1 feather (shapeless).
Mixtape Case - 1 minigame case, 1 black dye, and 1 redstone dust (shapeless).
Molecano - 1 minigame case, 1 lava bucket, and 1 minecart (shapeless; a bucket is returned).
Octeaparty - 1 minigame case, 1 sugar, and 1 water bucket (shapeless; a bucket is returned).
Pantry Parade - 1 minigame case, 1 glow berries, and 1 sweet berries (shapeless).
Pop-Up Kitchen - 1 minigame case, 1 stick, and 1 fish (shapeless).
President Bird - 1 minigame case, 1 feather, and 1 light blue dye (shapeless).
Rock, Paper, Showdown! - 1 minigame case, 1 paper, 1 shears, and 1 stone/cobblestone (shapeless).
Snakedown - 1 minigame case, 1 decorated pot, and 1 gold ingot (shapeless).
Steady Bears - 1 minigame case, 1 iron ingot, 1 wool, and 1 ice/packed ice/blue ice (shapeless).
Sweet Tooth - 1 minigame case and 1 cake (shapeless).
