Moles (Bits & Bops)

The moles are a pair of moles in Bits & Bops, appearing within the minigame/mixtapes Molecano, Ocean Mixtape, and Final Mixtape. They work as partners to operate a handcar to extract crystals from a cave in a volcano. The moles have brown, rotund bodies with short arms and legs. They have three fingers and lighter coloration at the bottom of their feet. Their mouths are below short snouts with dark noses, and they have floppy ears towards the back of their heads. Their eyes are partially obscured by the yellow mining helmets they wear, which are fully revealed while in the active part of the volcano. During the Ocean Mixtape, they have icy off-white bodies with silver mining helmets.

When one mole lands, the other is flung in the air, before slamming back down to push their side of the handcar's handle. In the volcano, the moles are launched higher, using their bodies to land on the hande.
