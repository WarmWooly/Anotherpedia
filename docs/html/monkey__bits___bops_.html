Monkey (Bits & Bops)

The monkey is a brown monkey in Bits & Bops, appearing within the minigame/mixtapes Snakedown, Fire Mixtape, and Final Mixtape. The monkey also appears on the title screen. He works alongside the snake to steal the snakecharmer's money. The monkey is brown with light brown hands, feet, face and ears. He has four fingers, three toes, and a long tail. He has a simple hairstyle with two stacked lobes.Outside of the tutorial, he dons a black bandana around his eyes, with a blue cap and a blue-and-white striped shirt. He also owns a smartphone, which he takes selfies with. In the Fire Mixtape, his body is tinted orange.
