Movement (Snaptic)

<>
Movement refers to the player control for Anon in the upcoming puzzle-platformer Snaptic. When falling out of bounds, Anon will be returned to solid ground. If Anon hits a surface very fast, an impact frame will occur. Full-body movement are the abilities Anon has in his full-body form. He can run at a constant pace and can perform a variable-sized jump based on how long the button is held down. He can change his direction in mid-air and create impact effects when jumping and landing. Anon can be forced into a ragdoll state, causing him to have less air control while airborne. Marble form can't be accessed while ragdolling, and when entering a ragdoll state, Anon will be forced out of marble form. Marble movement covers Anon going in and out of his marble form and actions he can take as a marble. He is much smaller in size and has a variable horizontal speed. As Anon speeds up, it takes more time for him to slow down. He also has less air control in marble form and can't interact with objects or NPCs.

He can still jump, can turn into a marble mid-jump, and can pop out of marble form in mid-air to gain an extra horizontal and vertical boost. He can only transform into and out of marble form once until he touches the ground again. Upon hitting or rolling alongside a surface, Anon can jump off of it for additional speed.

As his full-body form has better air control than marble form, Anon can cross large distances as a marble before reverting to adjust their landing position. As stairs are internally slopes, Anon can roll up and down stairs in marble form. Anon can enter vases while in marble form, which transport him to a new place. In marble form, Anon will be attracted towards magnet walls. He will stick to and slightly bounce off of magnet walls. If the player stops holding a direction while Anon is on a magnet wall, Anon will stop moving almost instantly. Anon can bounce off the wall while jumping, gaining more height until he is fully free from the wall. Exiting marble mode also stops Anon from being attracted to it. Landing on a magnet wall counts as being grounded, allowing Anon to go in and out of marble form repeatedly.

Magnet walls may also appear in the form of spheres, in which the camera will be locked onto the center of the magnet sphere. Anon can fully roll around the sphere in marble form and can stand on it while in full-body form. There are clear tubes that Anon can only enter in marble form. These tubes will slow Anon while going upward and speed him up while going downward. If the tube is mostly flat, Anon can control his direction. Anon can traverse a tube that is going upward if he has enough speed, but he doesn't gain speed while trying to go upward. He can't exit marble form while inside a tube. Magnet walls can pull Anon while inside a tube, allowing Anon to be attracted and launched through the tube. Anon can push around physics objects by running into them, regardless of form. While the full-body form offers more precision and control, marble form allows Anon to slam into physics objects to push them further. Some physics objects may even be draggable, which can push Anon around. As draggable objects can be moved quickly, they can be used to launch Anon while in marble form. At the end of a secret subarea in the demo, Anon himself can be dragged around in the ragdoll state.
