Mr. President (Bits & Bops)

Mr. President is a red parrot in Bits & Bops, appearing within the minigame/mixtapes President Bird, Fire Mixtape, and Final Mixtape. Mr. President also appears in Encore! He serves as the president, publicly addressing the nation with the help of his aide. Mr. President is red in color, with his tail feathers and outer parts of his wings being blue. His feet and beak are yellow-orange. He has small eyes with large pupils on the sides of his head. He also has a small cyan necktie affixed to his chest. In the Fire Mixtape, Mr. President's tail feathers, outer parts of his wings, and necktie are green, with his eyes becoming yellow.
