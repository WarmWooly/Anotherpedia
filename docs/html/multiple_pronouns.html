Multiple pronouns

Multiple pronouns refers to the use of multiple gender pronouns, generally a combination or two or three from he/him, she/her, and/or they/them. Use of each pronoun may be context-specific (such as in some gender fluid individuals) or interchanged freely. The order of the pronouns is based on the person's preference. Rarely, multiple pronouns will include neopronouns or it.
