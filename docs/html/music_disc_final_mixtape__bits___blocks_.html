Music Disc Final Mixtape (Bits & Blocks)

The music disc "Final Mixtape" is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks, based on the song composed by Zorsy. Music disc "Final Mixtape" can be crafted with 1 Final Mixtape and 1 vanilla music disc (shapeless). When used on a jukebox, the disc will play "Final Mixtape". The song has a duration of 3:37.
