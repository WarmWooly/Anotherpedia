Music Disc Fire Mixtape (Bits & Blocks)

The music disc "Fire Mixtape" is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks, based on the song composed by Zorsy. Music disc "Fire Mixtape" can be crafted with 1 Fire Mixtape and 1 vanilla music disc (shapeless). When used on a jukebox, the disc will play "Fire Mixtape". The song has a duration of 2:30.
