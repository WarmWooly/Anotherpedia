Music Disc Globe Trotters (Bits & Blocks)

The music disc "Globe Trotters" is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks, based on the song composed by Zorsy. Music disc "Globe Trotters" can be crafted with 1 Globe Trotters and 1 vanilla music disc (shapeless). When used on a jukebox, the disc will play "Globe Trotters". The song has a duration of 1:28.
