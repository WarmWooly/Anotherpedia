Music Disc Hammer Time! (Bits & Blocks)

The music disc "Hammer Time!" is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks, based on the song composed by Zorsy. Music disc "Hammer Time!" can be crafted with 1 Hammer Time! and 1 vanilla music disc (shapeless). When used on a jukebox, the disc will play "Hammer Time!". The song has a duration of 1:50.
