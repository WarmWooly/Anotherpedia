Music Disc Meet & Tweet (Bits & Blocks)

The music disc "Meet & Tweet" is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks, based on the song composed by Zorsy. Music disc "Meet & Tweet" can be crafted with 1 Meet & Tweet and 1 vanilla music disc (shapeless). When used on a jukebox, the disc will play "Meet & Tweet". The song has a duration of 1:30.
