Music Disc Octeaparty (Bits & Blocks)

The music disc "Octeaparty" is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks, based on the song composed by Em aka Lich. Music disc "Octeaparty" can be crafted with 1 Octeaparty and 1 vanilla music disc (shapeless). When used on a jukebox, the disc will play "Octeaparty". The song has a duration of 1:58.
