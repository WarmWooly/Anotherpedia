Music Disc Pantry Parade (Bits & Blocks)

The music disc "Pantry Parade" is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks, based on the song composed by Zorsy. Music disc "Pantry Parade" can be crafted with 1 Pantry Parade and 1 vanilla music disc (shapeless). When used on a jukebox, the disc will play "Pantry Parade". The song has a duration of 1:30.
