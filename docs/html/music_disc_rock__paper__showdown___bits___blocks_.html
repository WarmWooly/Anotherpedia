Music Disc Rock, Paper, Showdown! (Bits & Blocks)

The music disc "Rock, Paper, Showdown!" is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks, based on the song composed by Zorsy. Music disc "Rock, Paper, Showdown!" can be crafted with 1 Rock, Paper, Showdown! and 1 vanilla music disc (shapeless). When used on a jukebox, the disc will play "Rock, Paper, Showdown!". The song has a duration of 1:29.
