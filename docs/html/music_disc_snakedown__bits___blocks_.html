Music Disc Snakedown (Bits & Blocks)

The music disc "Snakedown" is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks, based on the song composed by Zorsy. Music disc "Snakedown" can be crafted with 1 Snakedown and 1 vanilla music disc (shapeless). When used on a jukebox, the disc will play "Snakedown". The song has a duration of 2:13.
