Music Disc Steady Bears (Bits & Blocks)

The music disc "Steady Bears" is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks, based on the song composed by Zorsy. Music disc "Steady Bears" can be crafted with 1 Steady Bears and 1 vanilla music disc (shapeless). When used on a jukebox, the disc will play "Steady Bears". The song has a duration of 1:58.
