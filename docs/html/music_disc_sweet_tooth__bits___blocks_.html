Music Disc Sweet Tooth (Bits & Blocks)

The music disc "Sweet Tooth" is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks, based on the song composed by Zorsy. Music disc "Sweet Tooth" can be crafted with 1 Sweet Tooth and 1 vanilla music disc (shapeless). When used on a jukebox, the disc will play "Sweet Tooth". The song has a duration of 1:50.
