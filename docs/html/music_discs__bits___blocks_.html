Music Discs (Bits & Blocks)

There are 21 unique music discs in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that can be obtained through crafting. All of the music discs are from the Bits & Bops OST, specifically from the minigames and mixtapes.<> All the music discs can be crafted by combining any of the minigames/mixtapes with a vanilla music disc.
