Muzzzzz's flow worm base is a NSFW flow worm character template created by Muzzzzz and posted onto Itaku, e621, and Bluesky on January 7, 2026. The base is designed for creating OCs of flow worms, both SFW and NSFW. It was most used during January 2026. Muzzzzz's design for the flow worms is distinctly off-model from their appearance within Bits & Bops. These differences include smaller, further-spaced legs, larger eyes, longer antennae and abdomen, and a pronounced muzzle. The base features basic customization, including multiple antennae and genitalia styles and addable wings and a halo to match the Sky Mixtape appearance.

The base also includes a color palette, modifiable background, and notes on the usual coloration of flow worms. It notes that flow worms have a brighter secondary color than their primary, and while glowing, are very saturated and have bright outlines. The "Space Fillers" depict various aspects of the flow worm, such as its full body and actions like singing and sleeping.
