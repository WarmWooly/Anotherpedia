Netherite Chainmail Belt (Not Lazy Chainmail)

The netherite chainmail belt is a belt curio in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail that applies Speed III, Haste III, and Slow Falling in the Nether. The netherite chainmail belt can be created through maille crafting with the settings "Netherite Belt". The belt takes 128 netherite chain links to craft. When the netherite chainmail belt is worn in a belt slot, the wearer will gain Speed III, Haste III, and Slow Falling while in the Nether. The Slow Falling is disabled while sneaking. Netherite chainmail belts can be crushed into unfinished netherite projects containing 128 chain links. gal
