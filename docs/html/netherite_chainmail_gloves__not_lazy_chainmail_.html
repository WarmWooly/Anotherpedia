Netherite Chainmail Gloves (Not Lazy Chainmail)

Netherite chainmail gloves are a hand curio in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail that sets hit mobs aflame. Netherite chainmail gloves can be created through maille crafting with the settings "Netherite Gloves". The gloves take 192 netherite chain links to craft. When netherite chainmail gloves are worn in a hand slot, they will set hit mobs aflame for 5 seconds. The duration will increase depending on the number of netherite chainmail armor pieces (helmet, chestplate, leggings, and boots) and/or netherite chain cover-upgraded pieces the wearer has equipped.
