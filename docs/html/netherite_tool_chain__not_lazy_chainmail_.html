Netherite Tool Chain (Not Lazy Chainmail)

The netherite tool chain is an item in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail used for crafting various tools and weapons. The netherite tool chain can be created through maille crafting with the settings "Netherite Tool Chain". The tool chain takes 192 netherite chain links to craft. Netherite tool chains can be obtained when a Crusher crushes a netherite whip, netherite flail, netherite lasso, or netherite nunchucks. Netherite tool chains are used in the following crafting recipes:
Netherite Flail - 1 netherite tool chain, 1 block of netherite chains, and 2 obsidian (place the obsidian in the middle and bottom left slots, the tool chain in the top right slot, and the block in the center right slot).
Netherite Lasso - 1 netherite tool chain and 2 netherite chains (place the tool chain in the top right corner and the chains in the middle and bottom right corner).
Netherite Nunchucks - 1 netherite tool chain, 4 netherite fragment, and 2 obsidian (fragments go in all four corners, the obsidian to the left and right, and the tool chain on the bottom).
Netherite Rope x4 - 1 netherite tool chain, 1 netherite chain, and 1 iron ingot (place the tool chain in the middle, chain below, and iron above).
Netherite Whip - 1 netherite tool chain, 1 leather, and 1 obsidian (place the leather in the middle, the obsidian in the bottom left, and the tool chain in the top right). Gold tool chains can be crushed into unfinished netherite projects containing 192 chain links. gal
