Netherite Wire (Not Lazy Chainmail)

The netherite wire is an item in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail used to be wound into a netherite coil. The bulk alternative to the netherite wire is the netherite spool. Netherite wire can be crafted with 1 gold wire and 1 netherite scrap (shapeless). Master-level chainmaillers have a 100% chance to trade 8 emeralds for 1 netherite wire. Netherite wire can be used on a winding jig or in an auto winder to be turned into a netherite coil.
