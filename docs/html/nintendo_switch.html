Nintendo Switch

The Nintendo Switch, or simply Switch, is a video game console released in 2017 by Nintendo. It features a tablet with a pair of thin controllers called Joy-Con that can attach to the sides. It can act like a handheld game console, where the user plays with the attached Joy-Con, or as a more traditional game console hooked to a TV. Two versions of the Nintendo Switch, Lite and OLED, were released in 2019 and 2021, respectively. The successor, Nintendo Switch 2, was released in 2025.

The Nintendo Switch returns from discs as used in the Gamecube, Wii, and Wii U to flash storage in the form of small, insertable cards. This allows games for the Switch to be easily stored and swapped. Content can also be downloaded to the Switch. There are also a Pro Controller and other accessories for the Switch and Joy-Con.
