Numel

Numel is a camel-like Fire and Ground type Pokémon from the third generation. Numel is a muted yellow in color, with a large torso, a neck extending to its oval-shaped head, and four stubby legs. Its snout and underbelly are tan, with a green covering on its hump. Numel's head has two large ears, a tuft of hair, and large eyes with dark brown around them. On the top of its hump is a hole, resembling a volcano.

Numel's official size is 2 ft and 4 in., while weighing 52.9 lbs. Shiny Numels are light brown and blue instead of yellow and green.

Numel is a portmanteau of numb and camel. Numel stores ~2,200 °F (~1,200 °C) magma in its hump and can transport up to 220 lbs (100 kg) of goods. When it gets cold or wet, the magma hardens, causing Numel to become sluggish. Its hump also shrinks when using Fire-type moves. Numel is mentally weak and doesn't notice when it gets hit. It is extremely driven to avoid hunger. A Numel has a 50/50 chance to be male or female. Numel is the 322nd Pokémon in the Pokédex and has the Pokédex color yellow.

Numel evolves into Camerupt at level 33. Numel's leveling rate is "Medium Fast".
