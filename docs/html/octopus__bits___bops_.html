Octopus (Bits & Bops)

The octopus is a blue octopus in Bits & Bops, appearing within the minigame/mixtapes Octeaparty, Fire Mixtape, and Final Mixtape. The octopus also appears in Encore! He runs the tea cart in front of a café, serving patrons. The octopus is blue in color with a tan underside and pink suction cups. He holds a teapot on his right, a sugar bowl on his left, with teacups on saucers in the middle. In the wide shots, he is seen wearing a cyan bowtie and a top hat with dark coloring near the bottom. In the Fire Mixtape, only the silhouette of the octopus can be seen, illuminated orange by the lighting below.
