Onychophora

Onychophora (also known as velvet worms) is the phylum under Panarthropoda composed of long, soft-bodied animals with many legs. They exclusively feed on small invertebrates and are found within Australia, Northern South America, and in smaller regions of Africa and Southeast Asia. They notably have slime glands that can be used to eject a clear, adhesive slime to trap prey and ward off predators. They engage in social behavior and can live in small, territorial groups.
