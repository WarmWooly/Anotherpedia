Pantry Parade (Bits & Blocks)

Pantry Parade is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks based on the minigame of the same name. It can be displayed on a minigame stand. Pantry Parade can be crafted with 1 minigame case, 1 glow berries, and 1 sweet berries (shapeless). Pantry Parade is used in the following crafting recipes:
Jungle Mixtape - 1 Pantry Parade, 1 mixtape case, 1 Flipper Snapper, 1 Sweet Tooth, and 1 Rock, Paper, Showdown! (shapeless).
Parading Legs - 1 Pantry Parade and 2 sticks (shapeless).
