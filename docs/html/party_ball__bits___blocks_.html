Party Ball (Bits & Blocks)

The party ball is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that spawns a smackable entity. The party ball can be crafted with 1 beach ball, 1 Final Mixtape, and 1 paper (shapeless). When used, the party ball will be placed. If the player is sprinting when used, the ball will be thrown. When the party ball is struck, it will be flung in the direction the attacker is facing. While it does have 20 health, it heals itself fully every tick. To kill the party ball, the attack would need to do 20 or more damage at once. The party ball will also float in water and lava.

The party ball is immune to fire, fall damage, cacti, drowning, lightning, potion effects, anvils, dragon breath, and Wither. If the party ball crams or suffocates, it will instantly despawn and drop itself.

The party ball can be picked up by sneaking while interacting with it. Unlike the beach ball or mangrove apple, the party ball will also generate orange, green, blue, and purple confetti particles when smacked.
