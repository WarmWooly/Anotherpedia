Patrick Shark Tank meme

The Patrick {{iShark Tank meme}} (also referred to as Pat's Spuds or the I'm Out AI meme) is an ableist 2020s AI-generated meme where a fictional contestant with Down syndrome on Shark Tank gets told "I'm out" during his pitch. It features Patrick trying to get the judges to support his potato chip business, "Pat's Spuds". After being told "I'm out" by two of the judges, he retorts before jumping into the judges. Patrick's condition is played for humor, with his assault directly tied to his Down syndrome. Reposts usually add captions joking about the situation and/or cut out the end where the judges call for security.
