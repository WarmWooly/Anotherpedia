Patrons (Bits & Bops)

The patrons are a set of customers in Bits & Bops, appearing within the minigame Octeaparty. There are 5 distinguishable patrons, among others in a crowd around and in the café. The patrons are served tea with sugar by the octopus. There are 5 distinguishable partons throughout Octeaparty, including beret guy, short hair lady, straight hair lady, sugar guy, and wavy hair lady (based on the in-game files).

Beret guy has light skin, orange hair, a tan coat with dark pants, a red scarf, and a green beret. He has a small beard with a styled mustache.

Short hair lady has fair skin, light brown hair, a purple dress with a matching hat, a pearl necklace, and a purse.

Straight hair lady has light skin, a pink dress and stockings, a yellow necklace, and a tan and pink hat.

Sugar guy has fair skin, gray hair, a cyan coat and top hat, tan pants and vest, a brown bowtie and belt, and a white undershirt. His hair extends down into his beard, with a mustache atop. He repeatedly asks for sugar during Octeaparty, looking happy after getting enough, or upset if he didn't.

Wavy hair lady has dark skin, brown hair, a yellow dress with a matching hat, and a yellow pendant. She has a freckle on the right side of her face.
