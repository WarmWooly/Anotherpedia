Perfect Tracker (Bits & Bops)

The Perfect Tracker is a score tracker for minigames and mixtapes in Bits & Bops, showing if the final score will be Perfect or better. It is unlocked after getting at least Amazing on all minigames/mixtapes, and is shipped in the mail as "perfect_tracker.exe". The Perfect Tracker can be accessed in the Settings menu in the computer. When playing a level without a Perfect, a crown will appear in the top-right corner, breaking when the player messes up. If the player gets all Perfect or better on all minigames/mixtapes, then the "perfect_updater.exe" will be shipped in the mail, allowing the player to view if they will get a Diamond Perfect.
