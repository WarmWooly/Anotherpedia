Persona

For other terms relating to persona, see persona (disambiguation).
A persona is the outwards, public-facing appearance of a person, often to obfuscate the personality, role, or identity. A persona is generally discussed in terms of one that is constructed by the person, which can be done to achieve a goal, fit in with a community, or to embody a character. Personas can also be employed professionally, like in acting.
