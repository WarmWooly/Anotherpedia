Plagiarism

Plagiarism is the action of copying work or ideas that the plagiarizer did not create, yet passing it off as their own work or ideas. Plagiarism is generally considered unethical, as the plagiarizer bypasses the work and effort needed to create something on their own without crediting the original creator(s). People caught plagiarizing often receive a punishment based on the circumstances, such as a failing grade or a suspension in school.
