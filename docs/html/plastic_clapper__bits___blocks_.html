Plastic Clapper (Bits & Blocks)

The plastic clapper is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that can be used to clap. The plastic clapper can be crafted with 1 Encore! and 1 magenta dye (shapeless). When used, the plastic clapper makes a clap sound. Yup, that's all folks!
