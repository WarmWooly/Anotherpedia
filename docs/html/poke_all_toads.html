Poke ALL Toads

{{iPoke ALL Toads}} is an indie puzzle game released by SpaceMatra on March 20, 2026. It features player-controlled faries who are required to poke all toads in a level. It has a pixel art style and a top-down camera. As players progress through the game, new toads and fairies are introduced with their own mechanics.
