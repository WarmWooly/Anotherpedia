Pokémon days

Not to be confused with {{aiPokémon Day.}}
Pokémon days refers to the fan-made holidays made to celebrate specific Pokémon, especially within Japan. With over 1,000 Pokémon, many days celebrate multiple Pokémon. On the flip side, many Pokémon do not have a dedicated day (i.e. Cradily). Some days do not have any Pokémon celebrated (notably Christmas). While fan lists have been compiled containing the most widely agreed-upon dates, the lack of official support can cause conflicting dates to appear. On a Pokémon's given day, fans generally share artwork and imagery of the Pokémon. People generally celebrate Pokémon days for their favorite Pokémon.
