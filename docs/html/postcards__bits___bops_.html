Postcards (Bits & Bops)

Postcards are a score sheet given to the player after completing a minigame or mixtape in Bits & Bops. Each postcard has a photo in the top-left, a sticker in the top-right, and a message along the bottom. The image displayed is based on the rating, with a bad outcome for getting "Try Again", a decent outcome for getting "Cool", and a great outcome for getting "Amazing" or better. Each minigame has a unique set of images, and all minigames and mixtapes have unique sets of text. Minigames can also display "snarky" text, which appears when the player purposefully messes up. The RPM will be written in the bottom-right cornerif the player is not on 33 RPM.

Postcards will be saved in a red album labeled "Memories" above the chair in the lounge. Any minigame or mixtape that the player has a "Cool" or higher on will have its postcard saved in the album. Skipping the mixtape with Bop or getting "Try Again" will not have it appear in the Memories album. Also, only songs on the default speed will appear, and custom mixtapes are not included. Each page in the album also has a unique color to match the theme of the minigame/mixtape.
