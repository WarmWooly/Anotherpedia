Preferred gender pronoun

Preferred gender pronouns (often just pronouns) are the pronouns that a person uses to refer to themself based on their gender. The most common pronouns are he/him and she/her, which correspond to the male and female sexes, respectively. The gender of an individual may not correspond to their sex (such as in transgender, non-binary, or gender fluid individuals), in which he/him, she/her, and they/them may be used depending on the individual. Multiple pronouns may be used to describe someone, with neopronouns and it/its also rarely used.
