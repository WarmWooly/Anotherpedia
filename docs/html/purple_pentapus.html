Purple pentapus

For the {{aiMagic: The Gathering card, see Purple Pentapus (aiMagic: The Gathering).}}
The purple pentapus is a fictional species of 5-limbed cephalopods (based around octopi) in Avater: The Last Airbender, first appearing in the third episode of the second season. They were mainly used to create noticeable marks for the fake plague "pentapox". The purple pentapus has a small, round body with five darker-colored tentacles round their bottom. They also have five eyes, with each eye along the side of the body, evenly spaced. The purple pentapus has a gray beak on its underside. Their suckers decrease in size from their body to the tips of their limbs, leaving a noticeable red mark on the skin. While they are difficult to remove with force, they can be easily coerced to detach by rubbing their body. They live in both fresh water and salt water, and can survive in sewers.
