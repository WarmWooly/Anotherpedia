Rainbow Skates (Bits & Blocks)

Rainbow skates are a feet curio in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that increases speed and movement control on ice while also applying a buff based on certain condtions. The rainbow skates can be crafted with 1 ice skates and 1 Sky Mixtape (shapeless). When the rainbow skates are worn in a feet slot, the player will accelerate faster on ice, packed ice, blue ice, and frosted ice. Sneaking on ice will cause the player to decelerate quickly. The rainbow skates are faster than the ice skates. They also apply Resistance II while on ice, at or above y = 192 (cloud height), or during rain/thunder.
