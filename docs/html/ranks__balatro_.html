Ranks (Balatro)

The ranks are the 13 alphanumeric denotations on playing cards. The ranks match the standard 52-card deck, with 4 of each rank in a different suit provided when starting a run. All the ranks have a corresponding base Chip value, which are the following:<>
The ranks are ordered highest-to-lowest Chip-wise in the hand. They can be split into three categories: numbered cards (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10), face cards (Jack, Queen, and King), and Aces. These categories, alongside specific ranks, can be used to trigger Jokers (such as Odd Todd giving +31 Chips on played 3s, 5s, 7s, and 9s). The Tarot Card Strength increases the rank of two selected cards, while multiple Spectral Cards can randomly add or change cards (notably Ouija turning the entire hand into a single rank with -1 hand size).
