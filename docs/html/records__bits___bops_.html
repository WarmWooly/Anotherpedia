Records (Bits & Bops)

Records are a music-controlling reward in Bits & Bops, which can be played in the record player. Most records are earned by getting an Amazing or better on a minigame or mixtape. All records from mixtapes have a unique pattern along the record. Records come in through the mail.

When a record is played on the record player, the music playing in the store will be of that record. The "Shop Theme" record will dynamically change between Bop's Shop and Bop's Longue based on the current part of the store the player is in. Some characters will have unique dialogue based on the state of the record player.

While most records are obtained by getting an Amazing or better on a minigame/mixtape, giving their corresponding record, a few special records have other requirements. "Title Theme", "Shop Theme", and "Tutorial Theme" are all obtained at the start of the game, and "Credits Theme" is obtained by completing the Final Mixtape.

The record player has multiple interactable elements. The tonearm can be lifted and moved to the side. If a record is inserted while the tonearm is still down, the record will scratch, with Bop or Bit telling the player not to do that. Repeatedly doing so will award the pin Scratch the Itch, with "Whoever scratched the records" added to the "No Thanks" section in the credits. There is also a speed selector for 16, 33, 45, and 78, with 33 being the default. If a record for a minigame/mixtape is inserted at a different speed, the minigame/mixtape can then be played at that speed, acting as a way to modify the difficulty of the minigame/mixtape when its corresponding record is unlocked. Different speed settings will have different rating stickers (such as an Amazing on 33 and a Cool on 45), but any non-33 ratings will not be visible on the save file.
