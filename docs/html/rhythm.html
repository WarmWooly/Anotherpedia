Rhythm

Rhythm is a repeated motion or sound over time, generally differentiated through a mix of strong points and weak points throughout the cycle. These strong/weak points can consist of an oscillation between states, with the duration of the strong and weak points defining the rhythm. In its broadest sense, nearly all interactions are rhythmic, such as a lifeform maintaining homeostasis, but it may be strictly defined to a deliberate system. Rhythm is often present within art, such as the flow of poetry or music.
