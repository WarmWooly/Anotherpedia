Rhythm Doctor

{{bRhythm Doctor}} is an indie rhythm game released on December 6, 2025, by 7th Beat Games. It features gameplay that utilizes a single button input, either by pressing or holding, to heal patients. The player is a remote intern who aids the staff at Middlesea Hospital, with each stage consisting of one or more patients who need treatment.

The game has a line that resembles an ECG chart, with beats traveling along it. The game primarily features beats that continuously travel to be hit on the 7th beat and pulses that have a delay before being hit. As the player progresses, new mechanics will be introduced that either obscure the beat or have beats travel at a different rate. Additionally, Rhythm Doctor will have the window change size and move around in some stages. The main game is split into acts, which primarily have multiple normal stages followed by a harder boss stage .
