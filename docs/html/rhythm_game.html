Rhythm game

A rhythm game is a genre of action game that requires the player to follow some rhythm, generally through input in time with music. They may require performing similar rhythmic motion, such as dancing for dancing games, playing an instrument-shaped game controller, or singing at the right pitch and volume. They may also appear as inputs timed to a beat on a mouse, keyboard, or controller. Rhythm games generally have an audio cue and/or visual cue for what inputs to make, with scoring based on how close the player is to the expected inputs.
