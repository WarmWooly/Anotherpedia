Ripe Tooth Necklace (Bits & Blocks)

The ripe tooth necklace is a necklace curio in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that buffs consumed fruits. The ripe tooth necklace can be crafted with 1 tooth necklace and 1 Jungle Mixtape (shapeless). When the ripe tooth necklace is worn in a necklace slot, the hunger of the following foods is doubled:
Apple
Enchanted Golden Apple
Glow Berries
Golden Apple
Melon Slice
Sweet Berries
