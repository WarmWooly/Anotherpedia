Roblox

Roblox is a free-to-play online game platform under the Roblox Corporation known for its custom game engine that allows users to create their own experiences (games). It primarily features user-generated content, including the experiences on the platform, clothing and equipment for avatars, and groups that users can join. While open to a diverse age range, Roblox is catered mostly to children and teenagers. Roblox also has its own economy based around Robux. A user can earn Robux through selling items, having users play their experience, as part of Roblox Plus, or by directly purchased. Robux, in turn, is then used to buy items for their avatar, to gain benefits in an experience, or rarely to access an experience.
