Rock Paper Scissors (Bits & Blocks)

The rock paper scissors is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that applies buffs when held in the hotbar or offhand. The rock paper scissors can be crafted with 1 Rock, Paper, Showdown! and 1 bottle (shapeless). While held in the hotbar or offhand, the rock paper scissors will apply Resistance, Speed, or Strength if it is in the modes Rock, Paper, or Scissors respectively. Using the rock paper scissors will rotate its mode. The rock paper scissors is used in the following crafting recipes:
Wild Rock Paper Scissors - 1 rock paper scissors and 1 Jungle Mixtape (shapeless).
