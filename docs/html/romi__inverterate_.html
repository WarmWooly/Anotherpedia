Romi (Inverterate)

Romi is a flow worm OC created by Inverterate, which was first posted to the Discord server for Bits & Bops alongside Blaze on July 14, 2026. For more images, see Gallery of Romi (Inverterate).
Romi has a light blue body with eggshell limbs/face and dark purple eyes/mouth. They also have a flat halo, with the sides lime and the inside seaweed. They glow a bright light blue with their halo colors flipped, as seen in an Easter egg for flow worms in Bits & Blocks. In their first appearance, they are seen talking to Blaze. The halo either appears around their right antenna or both antennae, with Romi's antennae color being fixed between their first and second appearance. The thumb is a canon thing Romi can do. Romi is considered to be chill.
