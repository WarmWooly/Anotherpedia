Romi (Inverterate)

Romi is a flow worm OC created by Inverterate, which was first posted to the Discord server for Bits & Bops alongside Blaze on July 14, 2026. For more images, see Gallery of Romi (Inverterate).
Romi has a light blue body with eggshell limbs/face and dark purple eyes/mouth. He (she/they) also has a flat halo, with the sides lime and the inside seaweed. He glows a bright light blue with his halo colors flipped. The halo appears around either one of his antennae or both antennae. The thumb is a canon thing Romi can do.

In his first appearance, he is seen talking to Blaze. Romi is considered to be chill and is a biromantic asexual. Romi tends to spend time in one place, taking interest in the things Blaze brings back. He dislikes loud sounds and can be easily angered, with his "angelic" status keeping him socially tame. He avoids swearing around Blaze and likes to tap his top legs together. It is implied that he used to have wings and lost them somehow. He doesn't interact with other Sky Mixtape flow worms, as the way they act makes him uncomfortable. Romi is attracted to Blaze, a feeling which is not mutual. He also seems to know Anno. He generally dislikes animal products outside of meat.

Romi is outwardly hostile towards Hippo, who has tried to "free him" from being 2D multiple times. Romi would stab Hippo to convince him to revert Romi to 2D.
