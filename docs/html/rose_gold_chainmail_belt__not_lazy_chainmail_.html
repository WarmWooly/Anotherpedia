Rose Gold Chainmail Belt (Not Lazy Chainmail)

The rose gold chainmail belt is a belt curio in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail that increases health. The rose gold chainmail belt can be created through maille crafting with the settings "Rose Gold Belt". The belt takes 128 rose gold chain links to craft. Rose gold chainmail gloves can be created through maille crafting with the settings "Rose Gold Gloves". The gloves take 192 rose gold chain links to craft. When the rose gold chainmail belt is worn in a belt slot, the wearer will gain Health Boost. The effect will increase depending on the number of rose gold chainmail armor pieces (helmet, chestplate, leggings, and boots) and/or rose gold chain cover-upgraded pieces the wearer has equipped.
