Rose Gold Chainmail Bracelet (Not Lazy Chainmail)

The rose gold chainmail bracelet is a bracelet curio in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail that prevents the wearer from getting Poison, Wither, and Bleeding. The rose gold chainmail bracelet can be created through maille crafting with the settings "Rose Gold Bracelet". The bracelet takes 64 rose gold chain links to craft. When the rose gold chainmail bracelet is worn in a bracelet slot, it will prevent the wearer from getting the effects Poison, Wither, and Bleeding until removed. If the effects Poison, Wither, and/or Bleeding are currently applied when equipping the rose gold chainmail bracelet, they are removed. Rose gold chainmail bracelets can be crushed into unfinished rose gold projects containing 64 chain links. gal
