Rose Gold Chainmail Gloves (Not Lazy Chainmail)

Rose gold chainmail gloves are a hand curio in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail that has a chance to heal when attacking mobs. Rose gold chainmail gloves can be created through maille crafting with the settings "Rose Gold Gloves". The gloves take 192 rose gold chain links to craft. When rose gold chainmail gloves are worn in a hands slot, the wearer has a 10% chance to give themselves Instant Health when hitting a mob. The chance will increase depending on the number of rose gold chainmail armor pieces (helmet, chestplate, leggings, and boots) and/or rose gold chain cover-upgraded pieces the wearer has equipped.
