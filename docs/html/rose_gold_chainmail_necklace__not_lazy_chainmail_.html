Rose Gold Chainmail Necklace (Not Lazy Chainmail)

The rose gold chainmail necklace is a necklace curio in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail that applies Regeneration when the wearer is hit. The rose gold chainmail necklace can be created through maille crafting with the settings "Rose Gold Necklace". The necklace takes 128 rose gold chain links to craft. When the rose gold chainmail necklace is worn in a necklace slot, the wearer has a 10% chance to gain Regeneration for 10 seconds when hit. The chance will increase depending on the number of copper chainmail armor pieces (helmet, chestplate, leggings, and boots) and/or rose gold chain cover-upgraded pieces the wearer has equipped.
