Rose Gold Wire (Not Lazy Chaimail)

The rose gold wire is an item in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail used to be wound into a rose gold coil. The bulk alternative to the rose gold wire is the rose gold spool. Rose gold wire can be crafted with 8 rose gold nuggets (place in a circle). Expert-level chainmaillers have a 66.67% chance to trade 4 emeralds for 1 rose gold wire. Rose gold wire can be used on a winding jig or in an auto winder to be turned into a rose gold coil.
