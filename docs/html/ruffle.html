Ruffle

For other terms relating to ruffle, see ruffle (disambiguation).
A ruffle, frill, or furbelow is a decorative trim to a fabric good, primarily clothing, that consists of multiple folds. The ruffle is tightly gathered or pleated to achieve the wavy effect. A flounce is a specific type of ruffle made by cutting a curved piece of cloth, which causes the fabric to naturally ruffle without gathering or pleating.
