Safety video (Bits & Bops)

The safety video is the rhythm game calibration used in Bits & Bops. It allows the player to set up important settings when starting a new save file, and it can be accessed again by ringing the bell for Bop. During the safety video, various options and calibration will appear. All changes in the safety video can also be altered in the settings. The first question will allow the player to disable/enable "Preview tutorials", The second question will allow the player to disable/enable "One button".

After, a metronome will play, with the player tapping to the beat to calibrate the input delay. If the delay is negative, then it will be set to 0.

Then, a simple tutorial will appear, with the player tapping on the third beat. Once the player completes it three times, they will be tasked with completing it with personality. It is made to show that the player should focus on playing to the music while hinting at how visuals in many minigames will change to throw the player off.
