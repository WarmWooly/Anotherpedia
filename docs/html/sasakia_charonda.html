Sasakia charonda

{{iSasakia charonda}}, also known as the great purple emperor or Japanese emperor, is a species of butterfly located in East Asia. It is best known for its blue-purple coloration as a butterfly and its designation as the national butterfly of Japan. The larva is a leafy green in color, with long antennae and distiguishable eyes and mouth. They tend to feed on hackberry trees. The butterfly has a pair of black wings covered in yellow spots, with the inside of the wings a dark blue or purple with white spots. The bottom of their hindwings has a red spot as well. The body is covered in black hair. Their wingspan is between 5 and 6.5 centimeters (2 to 2.5 inches).
