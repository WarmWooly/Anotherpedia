SCP Architect X

{{iSCP Architect X}} is a 2023 SCP Roblox tycoon game developed by SCP Architect where players manage a facility of SCPs to earn money. The player must create and manage living quarters for their staff, Class D, and SCPs as needed. As the player progresses, they will research new structures and procedures to hire new staff and support more dangerous SCPs, which in turn earn more money over time. It also features a system to color and decorate the facility, allowing players to express themselves.
