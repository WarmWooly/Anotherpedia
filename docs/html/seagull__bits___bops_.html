Seagull (Bits & Bops)

The seagull is a seagull in Bits & Bops, appearing within the minigame Flipper Snapper. They take over getting photos of the seal while the cat lounges partway through the minigame. The seagull is white with gray wings. It has orange feet and an orange beak with black dot eyes. Once the cat returns, the seagull is seen flying off in the distance.
