Seal (Bits & Bops)

The seal is a purple sea lion in Bits & Bops, appearing within the minigame/mixtapes Flipper Snapper, Jungle Mixtape, and Final Mixtape. He also appears on the holiday photos souvenir. He performs with a beach ball and gets his photo taken by the cat. Despite having the physical characteristics of a sea lion (most notably their ear flaps, upright pose, and their barking), he is referred to as a 'seal' in all contexts in-game. He has a purple body with three ridges per flipper. He has a large, dark nose with dot eyes and a mouth, with his ears towards the top of their head. In the Jungle Mixtape, the seal has a brown body with a lighter underbelly, starting from the bottom of their nose and tapering near his tail.
