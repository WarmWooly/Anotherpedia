Seconds Until You Die

{{iSeconds Until You Die}} is a 2026 indie survival game created by the YouTuber Fingees that is currently in early access <>. The game has a low-poly art style, with gameplay focused on adding seconds to a timer that constantly ticks down. When the timer expires, the player dies. Some actions, like consuming high-quality food and water and sacrificing animals, add seconds to the player's timer. Getting injured removes seconds from the player's timer. Fingees has a series of videos showing absurd updates to the game, often based on popular properties (The Backrooms and the End) or controversial topics (9/11).
