Sewaddle

Sewaddle is a caterpillar-like Bug and Grass type Pokémon from the fifth generation. Sewaddle has a similar shape to an infant, having a large head conntected to an oblong body. Its head is yellow, with a pair of round nubs on the top, large black eyes with a white center, and an orange oval snout with a triangular section removed from the bottom. Under its snout is a pair of pointy teeth on the edges of its mouth. A white underbelly extends under its snout. Its body has six orange nub legs. Sewaddle sports a green leaf garb, fully covering its body (not including the legs) and wrapping loosely around the back of its head. The head wrapping has holes and indents.

Sewaddle's official size is 1 ft, while weighing 5.5 lbs. Shiny Sewaddles have green bodies instead of yellow, with darker leaves and limbs/snout.

Sewaddle's name is likely a portmanteau of sew and waddle or swaddle. Sewaddle makes its own body wrapping, outside of the first one given to it by its Leavanny after hatching. It uses a sticky thread from its mouth to sew leaves together as clothing, attempting to mimic the same style that its Leavanny donned it in. Sewaddle uses its hood to cover its face while sleeping. The head nubs are sensory organs, with Sewaddles rubbing them together to greet each other. Due to its natural tendency to make its own clothing, it is often used as a mascot for fashion designers. A Sewaddle has a 50/50 chance to be male or female. Sewaddle is the 540th Pokémon in the Pokédex and has the Pokédex color yellow.

Sewaddle evolves into Swadloon at level 20, and with high enough friendship, Leavanny. Sewaddle's leveling rate is "Medium Slow".
