Silhouetted Tentacles (Bits & Blocks)

Silhouetted tentacles are a feet curio in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that doubles the duration of potions. The silhouetted tentacles can be crafted with 1 tentacles and 1 Fire Mixtape (shapeless). When the silhouetted tentacles are worn in a feet slot, drinking a potion will have the duration of its effects doubled. This only works on potions drank, so effects from splash or lingering potions will have their normal duration.
