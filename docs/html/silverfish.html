Silverfish

Silverfish are small and wingless nocturnal insects notable for their silver color. Its body is made up of multiple scales (hence the 'fish' part of its name), with tan legs, long antennae, and three caudal filaments (tail protrusions). Their scientific name is Lepisma saccharinum. Silverfish are considered a common household pest, often getting into food and damaging other goods within a home.
