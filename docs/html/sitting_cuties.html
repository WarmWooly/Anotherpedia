Sitting Cuties

Sitting Cuties (also called Pokémon Fit) is a plush collection sold by the Pokémon Center, comprised of small stuffed animals of various Pokémon. They typically have weighted stuffing beads at the bottom so they sit upright. Sitting Cuties are usually released in distinct series, generally split by generation. The first release featured 30 Gen 1 Pokémon in 2018, with new releases ongoing <>.
