Small Tie (Bits & Blocks)

The small tie is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that applies buffs to a parrot. The small tie can be crafted with 1 President Bird and 1 leather (shapeless). When the small tie is used on a parrot, it will consume the tie and permanently apply Health Boost (+2 hearts) and Regeneration. The small tie can't be applied if one is already on the parrot. The small tie is used in the following crafting recipes:
Burnt Small Tie - 1 small tie and 1 Fire Mixtape (shapeless).
