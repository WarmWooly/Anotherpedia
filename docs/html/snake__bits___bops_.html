Snake (Bits & Bops)

The snake is a green cobra in Bits & Bops, appearing within the minigame/mixtapes Snakedown, Fire Mixtape, and Final Mixtape. They work alongside the monkey to steal the snakecharmer's money. The snake is green with a tan underbelly and a pair of blue oval markings on their front side. Along the back of their head, they also have two green ovals connected with a tan pattern. Their eyes are red, and their tongue is blue. Outside of the tutorial, they don a black bandana around their eyes. They reside in a basket, using their head and body to bounce coins and gems with the lid. In the Fire Mixtape, their body is black, with a gold underside, orange/red markings, and a pink tongue.
