Snakecharmer (Bits & Bops)

The snakecharmer is a performer in Bits & Bops, appearing within the minigame/mixtapes Snakedown, Fire Mixtape, and Final Mixtape. The snakecharmer also appears in Encore! and on the title screen. He works with the monkey and snake, before getting robbed after keeping all the earnings for himself. The snakecharmer has dark skin with black hair. In the tutorial, he wears a white robe, yellow pants and turban, and dark green footwear. During Snakedown and the Final Mixtape, he wears purple pajamas and a matching nightcap. He is also wearing slippers in the shape of Bop. In the Fire Mixtape, their body is tinted orange while he wears a set of red pajamas and a nightcap.
