Snaptic

For the content creator, see snaptic (content creator).
{{iSnaptic}} (written as SNAPTIC) is an upcoming 3D puzzle-platformer developed by snaptic and Ziblo. It is based on the player, a metallic figure called Anon, traversing a 2007 offline laptop to reconnect it to Wi-Fi. The game also features two other known characters <>: Valence and Auralyn. Anon is shown being able to turn into a ball that can be attracted to magnetic surfaces, use other programs to interact with the world, and solve puzzles.

Snaptic is based on the same art style used by snaptic in his animations, with pixelated 3D rendering being the main visual style. It also has live action footage and screen recordings, which may play a larger role in the full release. It first had an animated showcase on May 17, 2026, with its Kickstarter campaign starting on September 29, 2026. After 6 hours of the Kickstarter, it reached its 60k USD funding goal.
