Soccer ball

A soccer ball is a spherical inflated ball used in the team sport soccer (association football). The size of a soccer ball is determined by the age of the players, ranging from as small as 18 in. to 28 in. The standard ball used in professional matches is the largest size (27--28 in.) Soccer balls are made to be kicked, having a rubber bladder to hold air and a foam, faux leather, and/or fabric outer layer. Various patterns and designs appear on soccer balls as decoration.
