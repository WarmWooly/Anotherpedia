Souvenirs (Bits & Bops)

Souvenirs are decoration in Bits & Bops, which are earned by getting a Cool or better on a minigame or mixtape. The Final Mixtape does not have a souvenir. All souvenirs from mixtapes are animated. Souvenirs come in through the mail. Some characters will have unique dialogue based on the souvenirs present.<>
