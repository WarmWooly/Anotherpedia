Spades (Balatro)

Spades
adeSuit are one of the four suits for playing cards in Balatro. The Checkered Deck has half of the deck consisting of Spades, making them more likely to be drawn. The Erratic Deck randomly selects suits, so Spades may be more or less likely depending on the selected suits. The Boss Blind The Goad debuffs Spades. Ancient Joker gives x1.5 Mult when a card of a randomly selected suit is played, including Spades. Castle gains 3 chips per discarded card of a randomly selected suit, including Spades. The Idol gives x2 Mult when a card of a randomly selected rank and suit is played, including Spades of any rank.
