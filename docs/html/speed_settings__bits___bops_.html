Speed settings (Bits & Bops)

The speed settings are a difficulty modifier to minigames and mixtapes in Bits & Bops. While records can be played at different speeds as soon as they are obtained, speed settings are only enabled after completing the Final Mixtape, in which Bit comments that Bop has noticed that the record player is affecting the minigames.

Records for minigames and mixtapes are only unlocked after getting an Amazing or better. To select a speed setting, the record for the minigame/mixtape must be playing in the record player at the correct speed. After beating a minigame/mixtape, the record player will be reset. Getting a new rating with a different speed setting will only appear when that speed is in effect, and it is not displayed at game completion in the save file.

Custom mixtapes also have a speed selector under "Modifiers" in 10% increments, from 10% to 1000%.
