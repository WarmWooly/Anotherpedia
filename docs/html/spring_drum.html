Spring drum

A spring drum or thunder tube is a percussion instrument that has a long, tightly-coiled spring attached to a drumhead on a long tube. The other side of the tube is open. The size and shape of the tube affects the pitch of the spring drum. It gets the name "thunder tube" due to the thunder-like sounds it can produce. Spring drums often have a printed design, such as lightning or tie-dye, with larger drums generally having an attached handle.

To play a spring drum, the spring is jostled, which causes the drumhead to vibrate. This can be done by shaking the drum, striking the spring, or dragging the spring. The spring drum is held with the opening upright, allowing the spring to hang downwards. A hand can be used to partially cover the opening to dynamically change the pitch.
