Squish the Bug

Squish the Bug is a green caterpillar created by caridescent, prominently posted on Twitter, Bluesky, Instagram, and TikTok. Squish is based on the green caterpillars that caridescent would often draw, with a finalized design made for her Makeship campaign in November 2026, which would pass its funding goal on November 17th. caridescent would also use Squish's design (replacing their head with hers) as a PNGTuber model while streaming. Squish is a green caterpillar with four pairs of legs. He has a light green body with dark stripes, and his first and third pairs of legs are also dark. He has a tan underbelly extending from his top legs to the end of his abdomen. Squish's antennae match the body's color and fold forward. His face consists of two black oval eyes, a smooth W-shaped mouth, and green blush marks.

Squish tends to stand on his back six legs, with his top two acting like arms. He is able to skateboard. Squish wants to become a plush since he wants to be more serious than a doodle.
