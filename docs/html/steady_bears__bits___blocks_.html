Steady Bears (Bits & Blocks)

Steady Bears is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks based on the minigame of the same name. It can be displayed on a minigame stand. Steady Bears can be crafted with 1 minigame case, 1 iron ingot, 1 wool, and 1 ice/packed ice/blue ice (shapeless). Steady Bears is used in the following crafting recipes:
Ice Skates - 1 Steady Bears, 1 iron ingot, 1 leather boots, and 1 light blue dye (shapeless).
Sky Mixtape - 1 Steady Bears, 1 mixtape case, 1 B-Bot and the Fly Girls, 1 Flow Worms, and 1 Meet & Tweet (shapeless).
