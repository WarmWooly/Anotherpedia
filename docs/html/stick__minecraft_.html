Stick (Minecraft)

The stick is a common wooden item in Minecraft used for crafting, especially tools. Leaves have a 2% chance to drop 1--2 sticks when they decay or are broken without Silk Touch or shears. Fortune increases the chance to 2.2% for I, 2.5% for II, and 3.3% for III.

When a dead bush is broken, it will drop 0--2 sticks without Silk Touch or shears. Fortune does not affect dead bush drops. Sticks can be crafted with 2 planks (stack the planks vertically; makes 4). They can also be crafted with 2 bamboo the same way, only giving 1 stick. Sticks have a rare chance of being caught as a junk item while fishing. The chances to find sticks in chests are the following (same for Bedrock and Java unless specified):
A bonus chest has a 100%(Bedrock only) chance or a 67.3%(Java only) chance of containing 1--12 sticks.
In trial chambers, an entrance chest has a 31% chance of containing 2--5 sticks.
In a village:
&tabA chest in a cartographer's house has a 26.3%(Java only) chance of containing 1--2 sticks.
&tabA chest in a fletcher's house has a 55.8% chance of containing 1--3 sticks.
&tabA chest in a toolsmith's house has a 90% chance of containing 1--3 sticks. The chances to find a stick in suspicious blocks are the following (same for Bedrock and Java unless specified):
In a desert well, a suspicious block has a 12.5% chance of containing a stick. Witches have a 33.49% chance of dropping 0--6 sticks on death. When slain with Looting on Bedrock, it can drop 3 more sticks per level (0--9, 0--12, and 0--15 for Looting I, II, and III, respectively). On Java, Looting increases both the quantity and chance of sticks dropping, with a 40.51% chance of 0--9 sticks, 43.83% chance of 0--12 sticks, and 44.91% chance of 0--15 sticks for Looting I, II, and III, respectively.
