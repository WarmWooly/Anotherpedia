Stroad

A stroad is a portmanteau between a street, with many destinations along it, and a road, which is designed to support lots of traffic. It is a form of arterial road in which businesses are placed alongside it, with frequent turning lanes and traffic lights to facilitate crossing to destinations. They are especially popular within the United States and Canada near suburbs, typically sporting gas stations, fast food, big-box stores and strip malls.

While stroads can support more traffic, their many destinations require more intersections to allow traffic to turn, which can cause stroads to back up. Stroads also directly make walking more difficult, as destinations are further spread apart. They pose a hazard to cyclists with poor to non-existent bike lanes and make it harder to install public transport such as busses, trams, and railways.
