Tempered Pot Lid (Bits & Blocks)

The tempered pot lid is a shield in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks, where blocking has a chance to deal damage to and burn the attacker. The tempered pot lid can be crafted with 1 pot lid and 1 Fire Mixtape (shapeless). Tempered pot lids can block like shields. If an attack is blocked, there is a 1 in 3 (33.3%) chance to deal 2 damage to the attacker and set them aflame for 10 seconds. The tempered pot lid can be repaired through combining by crafting, using a grindstone, or using an anvil. Combining tempered pot lids will add the two durability values together with an additional 3 durability. Combining will remove all enchantments, with some experience returned when combining in a grindstone. Combining in an anvil will keep and combine the enchantments on the tempered pot lids, costing experience. Tempered pot lids can be repaired in an anvil using an iron ingot, restoring 25% of the tempered pot lid's durability, rounded down. Tempered pot lids can be enchanted with Unbreaking, Mending, and Curse of Vanishing using an enchanted book on an anvil.
