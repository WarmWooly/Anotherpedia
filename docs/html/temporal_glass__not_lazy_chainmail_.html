Temporal Glass (Not Lazy Chainmail)

Temporal glass is a light-emitting translucent block in the Minecraft mod Not Lazy Chainmail. Any tool can be used to mine temporal glass, but the tool must have Silk Touch to collect the temporal glass. Temporal sand can be smelted in a furnace to produce 1 temporal glass, alongside 1 experience. A Cobblestone Generator with an end'n'crush'n'smeltifier will produce temporal glass. End'n'crush'n'smeltifiers take 128x as long to generate one temporal glass compared to one cobblestone. Temporal glass is used in the following crafting recipes:
Temporal Glass Pane x16 - 6 temporal glass (fill the middle and bottom rows).
