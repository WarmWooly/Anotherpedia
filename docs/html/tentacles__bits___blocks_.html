Tentacles (Bits & Blocks)

Tentacles are a feet curio in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that allows the player to eat sugar. The tentacles can be crafted with 1 Octeaparty and 1 (glow) ink sac (shapeless). When the tentacles are worn in a feet slot, using sugar will cause it to be consumed, giving 2 hunger and 2 saturation. If the tooth necklace is also worn, then it will give 4 hunger and 2 saturation. The sugar will go on cooldown for 8 ticks after eating. Tentacles are used in the following crafting recipes:
Silhouetted Tentacles - 1 tentacles and 1 Fire Mixtape (shapeless).
