The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild

{{iThe Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild}} (shortened to {{iBreath of the Wild}} or {{iBotW}}) is an open world action-adventure game in The Legend of Zelda franchise, released by Nintendo in 2017 for the Wii U, Nintendo Switch, and Nintendo Switch 2. The game has the player Link explore Hyrule to save Zelda from Calamity Ganon. They do so by harvesting items, completing puzzles in shrines to gain hearts and stamina, collecting and purchasing equipment and armor, and completing quests until they feel ready to take on Ganon. The sequel, The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom, would be released in 2023.
