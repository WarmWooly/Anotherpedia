Three-Legged Race (Bits & Blocks)

Three-Legged Race is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks based on the cartridge of the same name. It can be displayed on a mixtape stand. Three-Legged Race can be crafted with 1 minigame case, 1 pair of golden boots, and 1 string (shapeless). Three-Legged Race is used in the following crafting recipes:
Leg Band - 1 Three-Legged Race, 1 leather, and 1 orange dye (shapeless).
