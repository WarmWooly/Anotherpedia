Three-Legged Race (Bits & Bops)

Three-Legged Race is the second cartridge in the game corner in Bits & Bops, following the Sky Mixtape. Up to 4 players can join as local multiplayer, where they try to complete a three-legged race. Each player controls one character, pressing Button 1 and Button 2 to step. If the player(s) walk too slow, too fast, or press both buttons at the same time, they will faceplant. If the player(s) loop all the way around the city (~500 steps), then they are awarded the pin Marathoner. Attempting the cartridge unlocks Pop-Up Kitchen.
