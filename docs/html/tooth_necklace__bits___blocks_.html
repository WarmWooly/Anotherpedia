Tooth Necklace (Bits & Blocks)

The tooth necklace is a necklace curio in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that buffs consumed sweets. The tooth necklace can be crafted with 1 Sweet Tooth and 1 string (shapeless). When the tooth necklace is worn in a necklace slot, the hunger the following foods is doubled:
Cake
Cookie
Honey Bottle
Pumkin Pie
Sugar The tooth necklace is used in the following crafting recipes:
Ripe Tooth Necklace - 1 tooth necklace and 1 Jungle Mixtape (shapeless).
