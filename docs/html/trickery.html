Trickery

For other terms relating to trickery, see trickery (disambiguation).
Trickery is the act of deceit, misdirection, or illusion, generally for the political or financial gain of the person performing the act. Someone who does trickery is often known as a trickster. Trickery relies on cunning, sleight of hand, and cheating while acting genuine.
