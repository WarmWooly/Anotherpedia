Trotting Antennae (Bits & Blocks)

Trotting antennae is a head curio in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that applies buffs when going between dimensions. Trotting antennae can be crafted with 1 Globe Trotters and 2 shulker shells (shapeless). When the trotting antennae are worn in a head slot, going between dimensions will apply Regeneration II for 30 seconds and Speed II for 1 minute. Trotting antennae are used in the following crafting recipes:
Blazing Trotting Antennae - 1 trotting antennae, 1 Fire Mixtape, and 1 blaze rod (shapeless).
