Troublemaker (Bits & Bops)

Troublemaker is a pin and completion status for minigames in Bits & Bops. The pin is awarded after purposely messing up during a minigame, depicting the wren angry. Each minigame's postcard will also have a line of "snarky" text that appears when the player is a troublemaker. While the player will usually be given a bad rating, some minigames (such as Flipper Snapper and Snakedown) can get a score higher than "Try Again" while also getting the "snarky" text.
