Tutorials (Bits & Bops)

For the opening tutorial used for calibration, see safety video ({{aiBits & Bops).}}
The tutorials are short tutorials before every minigame in Bits & Bops. When playing a minigame for the first time, the player must complete the tutorial. Subsequently, the player must press Button 2 to skip the tutorial when playing a minigame. Tutorials may also set up the story behind a minigame, such as showing how the chef is a new hire in Pop-Up Kitchen.
