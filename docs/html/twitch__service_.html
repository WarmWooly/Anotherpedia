Twitch (service)

Twitch is a livestreaming service founded in 2011, being a spin-off of Justin.tv, and is currently owned by Amazon <>. Its main feature is a live chat where users can interact with the person streaming. Twitch is especially popular for streaming video games, particularly esports. Twitch also features messaging and video on demand. Someone who streams on Twitch is known as a Twitch streamer.
