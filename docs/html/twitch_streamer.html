Twitch streamer

A Twitch streamer (often shortened to streamer) is a content creator on Twitch and is a type of influencer. Twitch streamers are people who livestream on Twitch, where users can directly engage with the streamer. While a Twitch streamer can be an informal title for anyone who has a Twitch channel, it is generally used to describe large creators who stream on Twitch as a job. Twitch streamers are also often YouTubers, as they can post VODS, clips, and edited videos from their livestreams onto YouTube. Larger Twitch streamers have moderators to handle the Twitch chat.

Twitch streamers who meet specific criteria become a Twitch Affiliate, where they gain ad revenue, Bits, and subscriptions. If additional criteria are met, they can be invited to become a Twitch Partner, allowing for higher tiers of subscriptions and additional interactions and privileges.
