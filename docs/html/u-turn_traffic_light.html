U-turn traffic light

A U-turn traffic light is a directional traffic light that specifically designates a U-turn to be performed. These are primarily for when a lane is designated for a U-turn at an intersection, in which the light pattern adds a U-turn into the cycle, usually alongside traffic turning left, straight, and/or right. The direction of the U-turn symbol of the light is dependent on the side of the road, with left-hand traffic having a left-to-right U-turn symbol and vice versa. U-turn traffic lights may also be accompanied by a sign. Some U-turn traffic lights also have an arrow pointing left or right to signal that it is for both turns and U-turns.
