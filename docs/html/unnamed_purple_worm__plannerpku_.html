Unnamed purple worm (plannerpku)

Unnamed purple worm is a flow worm OC created by plannerpku, which was first posted to the Discord server for Bits & Bops on February 6, 2026. He would primarily be used in various flow worm artworks on the Discord server, alongside appearing in the background of the Bits & Bops Reanimated video for the Final Mixtape. The unnamed purple worm, as the name suggests, is a purple flow worm with a light purple secondary color and black eyes/mouth. While glowing, he is a lighter purple. He is often shown wearing a red scarf with white lines, which tend to cover his upper legs. He is stoic and deadpan, and generally doesn't smile.

Despite the fact that the first post of the worm was for getting suggestions for the worm's name (which includes "Hyper", "Vast Grub", and "Tanner"), he is still unnamed <>.
