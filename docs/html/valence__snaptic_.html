Valence (Snaptic)

Valence is a shopkeeper in the upcoming puzzle-platformer Snaptic. He is voiced by Jacob Takanashi and first appeared in "glass memory". Valence has a gray, multi-layered torso, with his legs, feet, hands, and head all slightly disconnected. His legs and hands are smooth and chrome-like, having four fingers and cuffs around his hands. His head is a white, thin octagon with two blue line eyes. He wears a white coat with a blue interior, blue and white shoes, and a white cap with a blue "V" on the front. Non-chrome parts of him are sometimes translucent, with a dark gray check pattern below. It is unknown how Valence got on the laptop, and Anon doesn't recognize him when they meet. At some point in the game, Anon asks Valence if he's met with Auralyn.
