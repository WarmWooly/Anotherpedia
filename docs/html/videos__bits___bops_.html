Videos (Bits & Bops)

Videos are recorded footage, either player gameplay or a cutscene, in Bits & Bops. Most videos are earned by getting a Perfect or better on a minigame or mixtape. The video "Intro" is obtained when the game is started, with the opening cutscene, while the video "Credits" is obtained by completing the Final Mixtape, showing the credits for the game. If a better score is achieved on a minigame/mixtape (such as getting a Diamond Perfect over a Perfect), then the video will update. Videos come in through the mail.
