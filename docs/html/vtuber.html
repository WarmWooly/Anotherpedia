VTuber

A VTuber (short for virtual YouTuber) is a YouTuber and/or live streamer (usually a Twitch streamer) who uses a virtual avatar to represent themselves. This avatar can be linked to motion capture to show the person's expressions on the model. VTubers can represent themselves as anything, such as a stylized human, animal, or object. Moe anthropomorphism, a form of anthropomorphism derived from Japanese pop culture, is often used to make humanoid avatars based around animals. A subset of VTubers are PNGTubers, which utilize multiple sprites rather than a rigged model.
