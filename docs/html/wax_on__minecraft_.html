Wax On (Minecraft)

For the achievement in {{aiMinecraft: Bedrock Edition, see Wax on, Wax off (aiMinecraft).}}
Wax On is an advancement available on Minecraft: Java Edition that is earned when the player applies honeycomb to a block made of copper. It is the child of the advancement Bee Our Guest and the parent of the advancement Wax Off. The description is as follows:Apply Honeycomb to a Copper block!There are no rewards granted upon completing the advancement.
