We're Not Candy

We're Not Candy was a 1980 PSA in the Kid's Corner episode "Jasper's Hospital Experience". It features four blue pill hand puppets, forming a barbershop quartet, who sing about not eating stray medication. After singing, red gloves put them back in the pill bottle and seal it. The PSA would be sampled for Busta Rhymes' "Dangerous" in 1997 and be parodied in the Last Week Tonight episode "Generic Drugs" in 2026. Hey, what are we doing out of the bottle?
