Wild Rock Paper Scissors (Bits & Blocks)

The wild rock paper scissors is an item in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that applies buffs when held in the hotbar or offhand. The wild rock paper scissors can be crafted with 1 rock paper scissors and 1 Jungle Mixtape (shapeless). While held in the hotbar or offhand, the wild rock paper scissors will apply Fire Resistance, Haste, or Regeneration if it is in the modes Rock, Paper, or Scissors respectively. Using the wild rock paper scissors will rotate its mode.
