Windblown Dancing Antenna (Bits & Blocks)

The windblown dancing antenna is a head curio in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that applies buffs while sneaking. The windblown dancing antenna can be crafted with 1 dancing antenna and 1 Sky Mixtape (shapeless). When the windblown dancing antenna is worn in a head slot, sneaking will apply Strength II and Resistance II.
