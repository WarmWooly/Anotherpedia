Wren (Bits & Bops)

The wren is a blue wren in Bits & Bops, appearing within the minigame/mixtapes Meet & Tweet, Sky Mixtape, and Final Mixtape. They are seen talking with the canary. The wren is a round, blue bird with small wings. They have purple, two-toed feet, a long tail feather, and a purple beak with dark blue dot eyes. The wren is somewhat bean-shaped. They are a wild animal, living outdoors. They like exercise, and enjoy singing and talking with the canary. They are also shown to have multiple children, which they care for.

Within the promotional material for Bits & Bops, the canary appears multiple times, both as serious assets and in memes.
