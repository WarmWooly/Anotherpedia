Wren (Bits & Bops)

The wren is a blue wren in Bits & Bops, appearing within the minigame/mixtapes Meet & Tweet, Sky Mixtape, and Final Mixtape. The wren also appears in Encore!, on the Troublemaker pin, and on the title screen. She is seen talking with the canary. The wren is a round, blue bird with small wings. She has purple, two-toed feet, a long tail feather, and a purple beak with dark blue dot eyes. The wren is somewhat bean-shaped. She is a wild animal, living outdoors. She likes exercise, and enjoys singing and talking with the canary. She is also shown to have multiple children, which she cares for.

Within the promotional material for Bits & Bops, the wren appears multiple times, both as serious assets and in memes. The wren appears in Rhythm Doctor, being treated alongside the Cockatiel in the crossover level Meet and Tweet. The canary also joins alongside the wren later in the level.
