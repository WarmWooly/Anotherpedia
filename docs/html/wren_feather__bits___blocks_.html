Wren Feather (Bits & Blocks)

The wren feather is a necklace curio in the Minecraft mod Bits & Blocks that applies a buff while the sky is visible. The wren feather can be crafted with 1 canary feather, 1 Sky Mixtape, and 1 blue dye (shapeless). When the wren feather is worn in a necklace slot, being in a spot where the sky is visible above the player will apply Regeneration II.
