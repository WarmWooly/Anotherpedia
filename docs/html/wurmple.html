Wurmple

Wurmple is a caterpillar-like Bug type Pokémon from the third generation. Wurmple has a red, spiky body with a smooth, segmented cream underbelly. It has 10 white conical legs along its body. On its backside sits two large, yellow spikes, with a smaller yellow spike at the top of its head. Its head also has a large red spike, two large yellow eyes with black pupils, and a vertical mouth down its protruded face.

Wurmple's official size is 1 ft, while weighing 7.9 lbs. Shiny Wurmples are purple instead of red.

Wurmple's name is likely a portmanteau with worm (same phonetically). Wurmple feeds off leaves and tree sap, with Wurmple using its rear spikes to peel bark off trees. Its feet have suction pads, allowing it to stick to smooth surfaces. They are found within thick grasses and within forests, being the prey of bird Pokémon like Swellow and Starly. It defends itself with its spikes, which can ooze poison, and its sticky thread it can shoot from its mouth. A Wurmple has a 50/50 chance to be male or female. Wurmple is the 265th Pokémon in the Pokédex and has the Pokédex color red.

At level 7, Wurmple will evolve into Silicoon or Cascoon (with the selection based on its personality value, making it effectively random), which will then evolve at level 10 into Beautifly or Dustox respectively.
