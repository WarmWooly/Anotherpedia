"YTPMV: YouTube is Where the Poop Is (Parody of "We Didn't Start the Fire") [Ft. Morshu]" is a YouTube Poop parody of Billy Joel's "We Didn't Start the Fire", uploaded to YouTube on October 21, 2026 by Silver C Stick. It features sentence mixing to make various characters sing the lyrics, primarily the announcer in Super Smash Bros. Brawl and Morshu from Link: The Faces of Evil. The video shows a loose timeline of various advancements and important moments among the YouTube Poop community, with a focus on the specific media that were used in YouTube Poops. It highlights specific YTPs, including "DINNER BLASTER", "Scrub Scrub Scrub", and "Big Beat Mario". Other media referenced include the video series Smash Bros.
