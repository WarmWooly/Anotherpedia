YouTuber

A YouTuber is a content creator on YouTube and is a type of influencer. YouTubers upload short or long videos and/or livestreams to the platform, which users can watch and engage with. YouTubers who meet specific criteria can join the YouTube Partner Program, where they can receive ad revenue and Premium revenue from users watching their videos. While a YouTuber can be an informal title for anyone who has a YouTube channel, it is generally used to describe large creators who post to YouTube as a job. Larger YouTubers may have staff to aid in video production and release products based around their brand. YouTubers may also create content on other platforms, such as Nebula and Twitch.
