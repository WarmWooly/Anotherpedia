Zelda's Pro Skater

"Zelda's Pro Skater" is a YouTube short posted by Kleric on November 15, 2026, in which Kleric has Link ride on a makeshift "skateboard" in The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom. It originally appeared as a segment from his "Spice Up Your Autobuilds!" YouTube video posted the day prior, specifically between 1:06:04 and 1:06:25.
