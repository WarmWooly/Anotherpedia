Zorsy

Julian Sanchez, known online as Zorsy or ZorsySan, is an Australian freelance musician and pianist. He has worked on several soundtracks for video games and films, including Steven Universe: The Movie, Vitamin Connection, and Bits & Bops. Zorsy also plays in the band The Consouls and produces his own songs. He wears a purple beanie as his signature piece.
